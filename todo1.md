## Assessment: Is the arc a complete circuit of AI mastery?

**Short answer:** For **deep operator mastery of AI direction and cothinking**, the arc is a real circuit — closed, load-bearing, and better than almost anything in this category.  
It is **not** a complete circuit of “all AI mastery.” It shouldn’t try to be. Measured against *your* mandate, it is **~85% closed**: the main loop is whole; a few outer loops are still thin or implied.

---

### What “complete circuit” means here

Given `MEMORY.md`, mastery is not model math, MLOps, or chat fluency. It is:

**direct → harness → verify → adjudicate → bound → choose machine → transfer**

A complete *operator* circuit has to return the student to work able to run that loop without the course scaffolding.

---

### The circuit you actually built

```text
B0  Direct mess → instrument + criteria
 ↓
P1  Make judgment products regenerable (machine, not answer)
 ↓
P2  Improve the harness on purpose (walls compound)
 ↓
P3  Multi-mind demand; own the verdict
 ↓
P4  Cothink inside *your* knowledge with walkable trails
 ↓
P5  Defend the operating picture (containment = absence of effect)
 ↓
P6  Autonomy under contract (command / exceptions / stop)
 ↓
P7  Discriminate agent loop vs pipeline + human gate
 ↓
P8  Method survives model/vendor/policy — transfer
```

With Move 1–2 wrapped around every node:

```text
LIVE brief  →  mission  →  log + verdict  →  mastery-floor self-check
```

That is a **holistic operator circuit**, not a topic list. Each stage needs the previous one:

| Without… | Later stage collapses into… |
|---|---|
| B0 direction | P1–P8 become tool tourism |
| P1 regenerable truth | P3/P4/P8 have nothing durable to adjudicate or transfer |
| P2 craft | Autonomy and transfer are rented personality, not owned walls |
| P3 verdict ownership | P5/P6/P8 become trust-the-brand |
| P4 trails | P5 poison has no “trusted picture” to defend |
| P5 containment | P6 autonomy is reckless |
| P6 contract | P7 discrimination has no agent pole |
| P7 machine choice | P8 transfer is one-shape dogma |
| P8 transfer | The week is a camp, not an operating capability |

So: **yes — as designed, the arc forms a closed cognitive circuit.** The raised MVP bars make that circuit *enforceable*, not merely described.

### Where the circuit is incomplete or thin

These are the gaps between “excellent operator school” and “complete circuit of AI mastery” *even on your own terms*.

**1. The human learning loop is weekly, not yet meta-mastered.**  
Students run act→observe→correct on missions. They do **not** yet have a deliberate block on *improving themselves as operators* — calibration, bias when tired, when to slow down, how their log quality trends. Move 3 (living 30-60-90) and a light “operator retrospect” can close this. Without it, the circuit trains performance inside the school more than self-sustaining operator development.

**2. Collaboration / institutional cothinking is light.**  
Most mastery is solo-with-machine. Real work is desk chains: handoff briefs, shared contracts, who owns the verdict when two operators disagree. P3 peer defense and P5 side-quest hint at it; it isn’t a first-class stage. For “complete” in org settings, you need at least one **shared operating picture** exercise (pair adjudication or contract countersign).

**3. Data / tool reality outside the sandbox is implied.**  
Messy folders, vaults, feeders, n8n — good. Still thin: permissions, secrets, retention, “this corpus is classified/wrong/incomplete,” live network risk. P5/P8 touch integrity and policy; **operational security and data stewardship** as standing craft are not a full node.

**4. Cost, latency, and capacity as operator variables are almost absent.**  
Mastery of direction without ever feeling token/time/cost pressure can produce beautiful but unshippable habits. A thin cost dimension on the case suite (time-to-accept, cost per regenerate, fail rate under budget) would close a real-world loop P2/P7/P8 currently leave soft.

**5. Creation vs. critical loops are asymmetric — correctly, but know the trade.**  
The arc is strongest on **verification, bounds, and judgment**. It is lighter on long-horizon *design* of novel systems (architecture of a multi-agent shop floor, productizing an internal platform). Mission Architect title suggests more design than the spine prioritizes. That’s fine for operator school — but then don’t market “full AI architect.” You’re minting **operators who can direct and govern thinking machines**, not platform architects.

**6. Move 3–5 are still not embodied.**  
Circuit *logic* is complete on paper. Circuit *formation* still depends on:
- living 30-60-90 (transfer muscle)
- measurement continuity across the week (one scoreboard)
- golden image (so mastery time isn’t install time)

Until those exist, the arc is a complete **design** of a circuit, not yet a complete **induction** into one.

**7. Nothing first-class on evaluation design as a reusable skill.**  
P2 tests and P8 matrices practice it inside blocks. “I can build a frozen suite and a scored set of fixed cases for a *new* work domain” is the meta-skill that makes mastery portable. It’s implied by P2→P8; not yet named as its own mastery claim.

### Completeness scorecard (honest)

| Standard | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Complete circuit of **operator direction + cothinking** | **Yes, with minor outer-loop gaps** |
| Holistic across judgment / craft / knowledge / autonomy / transfer | **Yes** |
| Complete circuit of **all AI mastery** (build models, fine-tune, prod ML, etc.) | **No — out of scope** |
| Closed loop back to the job | **Designed yes (P8 + seeds); not proven until Move 3 lives** |
| Internally coherent (each stage enables the next) | **Strong** |
| Enforceable (bars match claims) | **Strong after the raise** |
| Sufficient for mixed backgrounds without diluting mastery | **Tension remains** — floor is high; facilitation must narrow *scope*, not *standard* |

**Overall:**  
**Holistic operator circuit: A-**  
**Complete for the mandate: A- / B+** (A- on design, B+ until transfer + measurement + self-operator loops are built)  
**Complete for “AI mastery” writ large: No, and it shouldn’t be**

---

### The circuit in one sentence

You take someone from **first directed instrument**, through **regenerable truth**, **crafted harness**, **multi-engine verdict**, **trailed knowledge**, **defended picture**, **contracted autonomy**, **machine discrimination**, to **method that outlives the model** — with cothinking and evidence at every turn.

That is a full operator loop. It is not missing a random Thursday topic; it is missing mostly **meta-loops** (self-calibration, institutional handoff, cost/capacity, portable eval design) and **embodiment** (30-60-90, week scoreboard, image).

---

### If you want the circuit truly closed

Minimum additions (not new project days — grafts):

1. **Move 3** — living 30-60-90 fed by P2/P6/P7 seeds, sealed at P8  
2. **Move 4** — one measurement spine students carry all week  
3. **Once-per-week pair node** — shared verdict or countersigned contract (30–45 min)  
4. **Portable eval named** — “build a frozen suite for your job domain” as explicit P8 (or P2→P8) mastery language  
5. **Optional thin cost/time column** on the case suite and hold/degrade  

Do those, and I would call it a **complete circuit of operator AI mastery** without apology.

---

### Bottom line

The arc already instills a **holistic, nearly complete circuit** of the mastery you actually care about — direction, cothinking, evidence, bounds, and transfer.  
It does **not** instill complete AI mastery in the universal sense; it instills something rarer and more useful: **the ability to run a thinking machine as a durable work partner under judgment.**

The remaining hole is not “add more tools.” It is **close the outer loops** (transfer habit, measurement continuity, operator self-calibration, light institutional practice) so the circuit keeps running after Friday.

## Design update (post-analysis)

**Golden image recommendation superseded.**  
Workstation setup is a **student pre-work module** (`prework/`): students install and configure vanilla Windows tooling themselves; Block 0 **verifies** the health-check gate, then First Light. Install experience is retained; it is moved out of contact-hour mass imaging.
