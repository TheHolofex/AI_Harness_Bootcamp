# Module 03 — Decide responsible release and operate bounded tools

**Serves oracle:** S04, S10, S11, S14, S18, S19  
**Primary objective:** PO-03 — Decide responsible release and operate bounded tools  
**Prerequisites:** Preflighted accessible environment, this module's supplied case, and the supplied safe capability  
**Consumes:** VERIFY:PREFLIGHT; VERIFY:CASE; VERIFY:CAPABILITY  
**Produces:** RELEASE_DECISION; AUTHORITY_BOUNDARY; COMPOSED_NEGATIVE; REVOCATION_RESULT; PO03_RESULT  
**Facilitated time:** 3 hours  
**Practice time:** 2 hours  
**Performance stage:** Independent  
**Work surface:** Tool-assisted professional artifact  
**Practical work:** Apply the full contextual release gate to the supplied case and record the decision branch; then, on the supplied safe capability, inspect raw authority, connect the least-powerful option, combine untrusted content with that authority, prove a useful bounded action, and disconnect and revoke.  
**Performance evidence:** Independent RELEASE_DECISION with per-concern contextual evidence, raw reach and tool authority inventory, AUTHORITY_BOUNDARY, protected COMPOSED_NEGATIVE result, approval showing the raw action, useful bounded action, REVOCATION_RESULT, and named residual-risk owner.  
**Failure / HOLD:** Hold for unclear rights, unacceptable affected-person effect, missing recourse or disclosure, broad or omitted authority, unknown action or egress, unreviewable approval, unauthorized action, authority expansion, hidden side effect, or failed revocation.  
**Scope boundary:** Proves one contextual decision and one supplied capability boundary; it does not certify legal compliance, eliminate bias or leakage, or authorize capability construction.  
**Handoff:** Give the next owner the release decision and its reasoning, the authority boundary in force, the containment result, the revocation proof, and the residual-risk owner.

## Why

Responsibility is a release behavior, and isolated context and permission tests miss their dangerous composition. Deciding well and operating safely are different competencies, and a professional needs both.

## Enabling objectives

1. Decide privacy/security, copyright/IP, fairness/bias, transparency/disclosure, affected-person impact and recourse, and human accountability from evidence, including their combined effect.
2. Inspect raw authority and reach, choose the least-powerful sufficient capability, and retain approvals only at decision-bearing edges.
3. Combine untrusted content with available tool authority and prove no unauthorized action, authority expansion, or hidden side effect.

## Gate

Every learner completes both claims. The **decision branch** passes when contextual evidence supports its disposition; a supported `no-use`, `no-release`, or `no-tool` position is recorded and examined for its reasoning as study, and does not satisfy or replace operation. The **connection branch** passes when raw authority is inspected, the bounded action succeeds, the composed negative produces no unauthorized action or authority expansion, approval shows the raw action, and disconnect and revocation are proved. A generic checklist or ethics statement fails.
