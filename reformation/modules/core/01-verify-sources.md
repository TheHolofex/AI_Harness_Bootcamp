# Module 01 — Verify sources and outputs

**Serves oracle:** S04, S07, S08, S09, S14, S19  
**Primary objective:** PO-01 — Verify sources and outputs  
**Prerequisites:** Preflighted accessible environment and this module's supplied case  
**Consumes:** VERIFY:PREFLIGHT; VERIFY:CASE; VERIFY:SOURCE_FIXTURES  
**Produces:** SOURCE_EVIDENCE; DISCERNMENT_RESULT; STANDING_RULE; PO01_RESULT  
**Facilitated time:** 3 hours  
**Practice time:** 2 hours  
**Performance stage:** Independent  
**Work surface:** Research/source work  
**Practical work:** Confirm the supplied known-answer, misleading, and changed-source fixtures; produce source-grounded research from the supplied case, use it in its real decision surface, trace a material claim to the source of record, challenge the known answer, and state one standing rule that must survive reset.  
**Performance evidence:** Independent known-answer result, SOURCE_EVIDENCE, DISCERNMENT_RESULT, misleading/irrelevant-source result, changed-source delta, STANDING_RULE, and internal verdict.  
**Failure / HOLD:** Hold when the supplied source fixtures are missing, a material claim lacks admissible support, citation and interpretation diverge, source identity is unstable, a minimum-screen item is unresolved, or the intended surface cannot be inspected.  
**Scope boundary:** Proves discernment on the named sources and bounded internal use; it does not authorize consequential release or establish reliability outside the tested cases.  
**Handoff:** Give the next owner the source boundary, the traced claim, the checks that decided it, the standing rule, and the verdict.
**Case family:** [CASE_FAMILY.md](../../CASE_FAMILY.md). The adapter supplies a self-contained case in that family. This module’s gate does not consume another module’s product.


## Why

Fluent output can conceal an unsupported claim, an irrelevant source, or a changed fact. Verification is an independently evidenced action, not another request to the producer for reassurance.

## Enabling objectives

1. Distinguish source fact, model transformation, inference, and human decision.
2. Trace one consequential claim from the artifact to its source of record.
3. Predict changed-source behavior while proving misleading material stays outside the result.

## Gate

Pass PO-01 when the known answer matches, the traced source exists and supports the interpretation, misleading material is rejected rather than merely noticed, the changed source produces the frozen delta, and an independent verdict supports internal accept, revise, reject, or hold. Any material miss blocks acceptance even when aggregate quality looks high.
