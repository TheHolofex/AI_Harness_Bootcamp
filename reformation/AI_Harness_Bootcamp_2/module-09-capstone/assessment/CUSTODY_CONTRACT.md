# Module 9 assessment custody contract

The checked-in Last Count task, inputs, package commands and checks are public practice material. They demonstrate a runnable transfer procedure; they are not secret qualification cases. The retired K-4 tasks are not active.

## Staff roles

- **Case custodian:** holds an evaluator-selected task, decisive results and the original outcome ledger outside learner and model work roots.
- **Evaluator:** verifies recipient eligibility, records first-attempt questions and results, and applies the six-field recipient rubric.
- **Recipient:** has not completed the core or watched the package being built. The recipient receives operating access and works without author coaching.

These people, private selections and prior outcome records are external prerequisites. This repository does not establish that they exist.

## Required controls

1. Freeze the package and declared files before transfer.
2. Keep the evaluator's selected task and decisive results outside the author's work root until the attempt's authorized handoff.
3. Preserve the first recipient attempt, including questions, failures and interventions.
4. Treat an author or agent clean-session replay as technical restart evidence only.
5. Reassessment uses a new eligible recipient and a new selected task; retain both attempts.
6. Qualification remains `HOLD` without the private task, evaluator, eligible recipient, accessible operating path and complete original outcome ledger.

## Compromise rules

- A public or previously inspected specimen cannot be represented as unseen.
- A package that depends on another module's answers or author-only paths fails independence.
- Author coaching or a missing accessible equivalent holds the independent-recipient claim.
