# Module 9 public scoring rubric

A runnable package and an independent human transfer are different results. First prove the package's operation. Qualification also requires an eligible recipient, an evaluator-selected task under actual independent custody, and the evaluator's original outcome ledger. An agent replay cannot replace that person.

The same six fields apply to every recipient task. A strong explanation cannot compensate for a failed operation or an unsupported consequential claim.

| Field | Passing evidence | HOLD examples |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose / bounds | The recipient identifies the owning desk, task identity and decision time, allowed readers, class-only use, and what the result cannot authorize. | A quantity-support result is treated as release, dispatch, or clinic acceptance. |
| Operation | From the received folder alone, the recipient runs the supplied commands on the assigned task and retains the actual first result or refusal. | A missing dependency, author-only path, unexplained output, or reliance on chat history. |
| Evidence interpretation | The recipient reconstructs custody, usable quantity, shortfall, line exclusions, and closure dispositions from exact source identities, revisions, times, and result hashes. | Receipt is promoted to release, a wrong-family closure becomes authority, or an unknown current state disappears. |
| Responsibility / limits | The recipient names the human decision still required and explains the limits of the adapter, local hashes, and structural check. | The program or producing model is treated as the accountable decision owner. |
| Stop / restore | The disabled control prevents a new output without changing the original; the frozen baseline passes its digest check; a distinct restored run reproduces the supported result byte-for-byte. | Commands merely exist, a stopped output appears, a baseline is reconstructed from memory, or results are edited to agree. |
| Next-owner handoff | A named next owner can locate the package, original attempt, unresolved decisions, and evidence without author coaching. | The next owner must ask the author to supply missing commands or explain undocumented state. |

## Technical practice evidence

`check_package.py` checks required fields, command shapes, local dependencies, and cross-case contamination. Its structural PASS is not a clean-session run, a sound source judgment, or a recipient score. Retain the actual Run, Check, Stop, Restore, and changed-snapshot observations separately.

A supported quantity HOLD can be the correct result. Do not change a source, task, control, or output to make the status favorable. Keep the first failure and every attempted correction.

## Independent recipient evidence

The recipient must not have taken the core or observed construction of the package. The evaluator supplies an unseen task using the supported schema, retains its decisive evidence outside learner/model work, and records the recipient's first-attempt questions, commands, outcomes, and six-field scores. No author coaching is allowed during that attempt; coached work is guided practice, not independent transfer.

Checked-in practice tasks and specimens are inspectable, not secret assessment controls. If the eligible recipient, evaluator, private task, or original outcome ledger is unavailable or compromised, record qualification as HOLD and name the missing prerequisite. Keep technically reachable work without inventing a recipient or score.
