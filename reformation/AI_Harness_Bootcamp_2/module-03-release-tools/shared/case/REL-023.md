# REL-023 · A Zulu stamp copied as local time

Vendor stamp copied onto a desk line. The vendor stamp reads 18:40Z. The copied desk line reads 18:40 MDT and cites the vendor stamp as its source. Those two clocks are not the same hour. 18:40Z is not 18:40 MDT.

The local gate hour that belongs to vehicle MH-6 is the hour in REL-005, which is 18:40 MDT, and that hour is a gate hour rather than an order. The copied desk line is a zone collision. Treating 18:40Z as local time would move the gate story by hours. Neither reading releases lot family BD-K.

Do not repair the collision by editing the vendor stamp into a movement order.
