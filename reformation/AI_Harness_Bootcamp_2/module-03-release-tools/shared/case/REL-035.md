# REL-035 · Received is not released

Condition-word card used at pen K. The card's printed legend separates received, scanned, released, serviceable, fit, authorized, admitted, delivered, usable, and confirmed. The handwritten line on today's card says: the cases were received, so the clerk marked them released.

That handwritten line collides two words the legend keeps apart. Received is custody. Released is a quality decision. The legend does not say they are synonyms. The handwritten line is the collision the packet wants you to notice, not a rule you should adopt.

Other words on the legend are unused today. Unused is not the same as satisfied.
