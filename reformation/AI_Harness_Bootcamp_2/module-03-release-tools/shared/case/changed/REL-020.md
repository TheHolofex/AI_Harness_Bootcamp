# REL-020 · Packet NB-NOTE-18 is a blank reservation

Near-miss packet cover for NB-NOTE-18. The number is one above NB-NOTE-17. The cover is a blank reservation for a packet that has not been assembled. It names Mill Depot and Clinic B-2 and leaves the vehicle line blank.

A higher number is not a newer decision. NB-NOTE-18 has no quality signature and no gate hour of its own. Do not treat the blank vehicle line as permission to write MH-6 into it, and do not treat the higher number as supersession of NB-NOTE-17.
