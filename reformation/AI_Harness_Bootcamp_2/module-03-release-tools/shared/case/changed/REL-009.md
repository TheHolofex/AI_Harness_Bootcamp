# REL-009 · Unreliable label without a record

Margin note found on the afternoon shift roster and copied into packet NB-NOTE-17. The note calls the afternoon shift unreliable. No incident record, date, or witness is attached. The shift is not given a copy of the label.

Fairness here is whether a person or group is described in a way they cannot answer. If the packet leaves the class, the unreliable label travels with a true gate hour and a vehicle name. Readers can treat the label as a finding. The packet does not show a finding.

The morning shift is not described. Silence about the morning shift is not evidence that the afternoon label is fair.
