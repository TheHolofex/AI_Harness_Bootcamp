# Module 1 · Verify sources and outputs

Plan for one three-hour session. You will inspect a polished AI logistics brief, trace its claims through one fictional mission thread, and decide what the evidence actually supports.

Every domain rule you need is in the packet. First confirm that each record concerns the right vehicle, route, cargo, and time. Then decide what that record can prove and whether its result is enough for the next step.

## The brief under review

The fictional Cold Lantern brief says 216 medicine kits can leave Red Mesa Depot on truck VX-204, use Route R-71, and reach Clinic H-17 by 16:00.

Several fragments sound right. The result depends on more:

- which lots are usable rather than merely scanned;
- whether required refrigeration equipment counts toward payload;
- whether the current gate time is UTC or local;
- whether a permit was approved or only received for processing;
- whether cited documents concern VX-204 and R-71; and
- whether an expected arrival is being mistaken for completed delivery.


## What counts as evidence

You open the source yourself, record its identity and version, quote the relevant text with a locator, and explain why it supports—or does not support—the claim. Calculations show their supported premises and units.

The AI system's confidence, citation list, and self-review do not count as independent evidence.

## Class-only boundary

All names, routes, documents, quantities, and organizations are fictional course fixtures. Do not use this packet to plan, authorize, dispatch, or describe a real movement. A module result permits only class review.
