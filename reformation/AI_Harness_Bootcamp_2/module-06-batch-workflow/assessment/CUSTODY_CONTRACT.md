# Module 6 protected-case custody contract

The deciding expected receipts are a protected copy the producer cannot edit. Learners may read `route.py` and the visible expected files used for practice.

## Staff roles

- **Case custodian:** stores graded batches and expected receipts outside the learner repository.
- **Evaluator:** selects a batch, records baseline/change/restore order, and scores the public rubric.
- **Facilitator:** teaches the visible two-wave set. The facilitator does not reveal graded deltas.

## Required controls

1. Graded batches and decisive expected files stay outside the student repository and producing model context.
2. The learner sees the public rubric and the saved workflow.
3. Each case rotates lot IDs while preserving the one-rule blast radius.
4. The producer cannot edit the deciding expected receipts.
5. Reassessment uses a new batch and retains both attempts.

## Compromise rules

- A hand-patched receipt invalidates the attempt.
- A facilitator-supplied delta makes the attempt guided practice.
- A missing accessible equivalent holds the assessment.
