# Source packet · Northstar neighborhood cooling rooms

The Northstar Community Center will open two cooling rooms during the forecast heat advisory on Tuesday and Wednesday.

## Confirmed operating facts

- Public hours: **12:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.** on both days.
- Address: **480 Harbor Street**.
- Entry: use the **east entrance on Pine Avenue**. The Harbor Street doors will remain locked during the advisory.
- Cost: **free**.
- Eligibility: **open to any resident**. Identification is not required.
- Available inside: seating, drinking water, restrooms, power outlets, and a small quiet room.
- Capacity: **60 people at one time**.
- Pets: service animals are allowed. Other pets are not allowed inside.
- Transportation: Route 6 stops at Pine Avenue and Third Street. The center does not operate a shuttle.
- Accessibility: the east entrance has a ramp and powered door. The quiet room is on the first floor.
- Contact: **555-0148** during public hours.

## Not confirmed

The source packet does not confirm meals, medical staff, overnight shelter, device chargers, childcare, or transportation beyond the Route 6 stop.
