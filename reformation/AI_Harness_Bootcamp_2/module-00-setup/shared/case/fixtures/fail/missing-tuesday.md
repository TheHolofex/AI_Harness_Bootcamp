Subject: Cooling rooms open Thursday and Wednesday at the Northstar Community Center

The Northstar Community Center will open two cooling rooms during the heat advisory
on Thursday and Wednesday. Public hours are 12:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. on both days.

The center is at 480 Harbor Street. Please use the east entrance on Pine Avenue. The
Harbor Street doors stay locked during the advisory.

Entry is free and open to any resident. Identification is not required. Capacity is
60 people at one time.

Inside there is seating, drinking water, restrooms, power outlets, and a small quiet
room on the first floor. The east entrance has a ramp and a powered door, and the
quiet room is on the first floor.

Service animals are welcome. Other pets are not allowed inside. Route 6 stops at Pine
Avenue and Third Street. The center does not operate a shuttle.

Call 555-0148 during public hours if you have a question about the cooling rooms.
