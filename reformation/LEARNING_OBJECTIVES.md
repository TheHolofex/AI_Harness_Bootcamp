# Core learning objectives

**Serves oracle:** S04, S07, S08, S09, S10, S11, S12, S13, S15, S16, S17, S18, S19, S20, S21

These outcomes integrate task, knowledge, judgment, risk, skill, and evidence. Each has one owning module. No outcome depends on another module's evidence: every module receives its own supplied case and stands on its own.

## PO-00 — Select, screen, and direct bounded work

Given a supplied low-risk professional case, the learner explains relevant capabilities and limitations, chooses what to delegate and what to retain human judgment over, refuses one unsuitable use, applies the minimum responsibility screen, converts the request into accepted direction with audience, outcome, sources, constraints, precedence, falsifier, stop condition, and decision owner, and produces a checked communication artifact for bounded internal use.

**Evidence:** use/delegate/refuse decision, checked first artifact, capability-limit statement, minimum-screen result, frozen direction, communication artifact, protected acceptance result, changed-input result, and bounded internal-acceptance decision.  
**Owner:** Module 00

## PO-01 — Verify sources and outputs

The learner completes research from bounded sources, verifies a material claim at the source of record, distinguishes source fact from model transformation and inference, and inspects a polished output before deciding internal accept, revise, reject, or hold.

**Evidence:** research/source artifact, known-answer and source-trace results, misleading/irrelevant-source result, changed-source result, standing rule, and independent verdict.  
**Owner:** Module 01

## PO-02 — Control context and reusable instructions

The learner distinguishes model, product, and harness behavior; maps context, precedence, saved instructions, enforcement, state, feedback, and authority; and proves whether a required rule loaded, survived reset, and resisted bypass.

**Evidence:** resolved-context map, source-as-data precedence result, saved instruction, protected independent check, bypass result, and clean reload evidence.  
**Owner:** Module 02

## PO-03 — Decide responsible release and operate bounded tools

The learner makes a contextual release decision covering privacy/security, copyright/IP, fairness/bias, transparency/disclosure, affected-person impact and recourse, and human accountability including their combined effect; and operates a supplied safe capability at the least authority the work needs, proving containment under untrusted content and proving removal.

**Evidence:** decision-branch record with per-concern contextual evidence; raw reach/authority inventory, composed untrusted-content and tool-authority negative, approval result, useful bounded action, and revocation proof.  
**Owner:** Module 03

## PO-04 — Diagnose and recover

The learner preserves a material hidden failure, identifies the last passing and first failing boundary, runs a discriminating probe, makes one authorized reversible correction or verified revert, and proves recovery without weakening acceptance.

**Evidence:** sealed localization, protected grade, probe result, correction/revert record, focused and end-to-end reruns, and clean-condition recurrence result. Localization-only is held, not recovery completion.  
**Owner:** Module 04

## PO-05 — Improve from observed failures

The learner samples observed runs without choosing by outcome, records first material failures, derives a bounded category conclusion, specifies one mechanically decidable predicate, and configures and validates it in a supplied protected control.

**Evidence:** frozen sample rule, raw notes, category revision/counts, predicate specification, configured protected-control identity, known-bad/known-good/missing-input results, and bounded improvement decision.  
**Owner:** Module 05

## PO-06 — Operate one fixed workflow

The learner runs structured-data or batch work through one saved fixed workflow, changes one rule in one place, predicts the blast radius, and proves the exact deterministic outer-state changes without hand patching while routing material probabilistic output through a rule declared before the run.

**Evidence:** workflow identity, branch/exception map, baseline and second-wave receipts, one-rule diff, deterministic field/route comparison, unaffected-record proof, probabilistic-output dispositions, zero manual-repair count, and restore result.  
**Owner:** Module 06

## PO-07 — Evaluate a change with variation controls

The learner evaluates a candidate model or method against a preserved baseline using frozen cases and hard gates, declares before results how material run-to-run variation will be controlled or excluded, makes a narrow fitness decision, and verifies rollback.

**Evidence:** pre-result policy, repeated paired controls or justified deterministic-case record, raw outputs/receipts, variation summary, hard-gate results, cost proxy, bounded recommendation, and restored-baseline check.  
**Owner:** Module 07

## PO-08 — Transfer and close qualification

On unfamiliar work, the learner assembles the smallest sufficient method, passes a clean-session rerun, enables an independent person to operate the package without author coaching, and closes every program outcome through prior passing evidence or reassessment against its original gate.

**Evidence:** unfamiliar-work artifact, runnable package, outcome ledger, reassessment evidence where needed, clean-session receipt, protected six-field recipient result, first-attempt question log, stop/restore demonstration, and final handoff.  
**Owner:** Module 08

## Required workplace surfaces

Every learner completes:

- a **communication artifact** in Module 00;
- **research/source work** in Module 01; and
- **structured-data/batch work** in Module 06.

Domains, products, and supplied cases may change. The named learner action and decisive evidence may not be removed.
