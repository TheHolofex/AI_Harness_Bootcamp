# Information need - September rotation route decision

**Requested by:** Task Group MERIDIAN, Chief of Staff
**Decision date:** 22 August 2026
**Rotation window:** 10 to 25 September 2026
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## The decision

Task Group MERIDIAN issues the September movement order on 22 August. The order
names one primary route for the rotation lift and one contingency. Two corridors
are available:

- **Route AMBER** runs north from Station Halberd through Checkpoint 2 (W-19),
  the Marrow Cut defile, and over the Selwyn at the Selwyn Bridge (W-41).
- **Route CINDER** runs south-west through Node D (W-07) and crosses the Selwyn
  by vehicle ferry at Ferry Point (W-22).

Naming a route that cannot carry the lift strands the rotation at a crossing.
Shifting to the other route without cause commits the group to a longer corridor,
a charter it does not need, and a staging fee at Node D.

## The question

**Which route can carry the September rotation lift as primary, and what would
have to change for the other one to carry it?**

## What an answer has to settle

- Whether the lift as composed can cross at each crossing, on the ratings and
  limits actually in force during the rotation window.
- Which restrictions quoted in current planning products are still in force and
  which are not.
- Where two sources disagree, which one governs and why.
- What contingency the other route offers, and on which days.
- What would have to change - and by when - for the rejected route to become
  primary.

## Corpus

`corpus/` holds the material the group has on file: structural and survey
reports, hydrology and meteorological series, movement records, standing
directives, contracts and correspondence, working group minutes, finance
planning figures, and open source material of varying quality.

Every claim in an answer must be walkable back to a file in `corpus/`.
