# CON-2026-002 - Charter, Ferry Point vehicle crossing

**Parties:** Task Group MERIDIAN and Selwyn Ferry Services Ltd
**Effective:** 2026-01-15
**Review:** 2027-01-14
**Held by:** Liaison Desk
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## 1. Vessel

1.1 The chartered vessel is **MV Tolland Carrier**, official number SFS-131,
operating under certificate **SFS-C-131**.

1.2 Certificate SFS-C-131 limits the vessel to **18 tonnes per vehicle** and 140
tonnes deadweight per lift.

1.3 No substitute vessel is chartered under this instrument. MV Corran Trader,
referred to in earlier correspondence and in the operator's brochure, was
disposed of by the operator in 2024 and its certificate SFS-C-114 has no
application here.

## 2. Operating conditions

2.1 The operator will suspend service when the Selwyn gauge at the Ferry Point
reach exceeds the limit stated in the operator's insurance condition, and will
resume when the gauge has fallen and held below that limit for six hours.

2.2 The crossing operates in daylight only. There is no lighting at the south
slip and none is provided for under this charter.

2.3 Suspension under 2.1 is not a default and attracts no remedy against the
operator.

## 3. Charges

3.1 A lift day is charged at 9,600 exercise credits whether or not the full day
is worked.

3.2 A **minimum charge of four lift days per calendar month** applies for as long
as this charter is in force, whether or not any lift is called.

3.3 Suspended days under 2.1 count against the minimum in 3.2.

## 4. Capacity

4.1 The operator gives no warranty as to vehicles moved per hour. Throughput
depends on vehicle mix, ramp condition and river state.

4.2 Any figure quoted in the operator's promotional material is indicative only
and is not a term of this charter.
