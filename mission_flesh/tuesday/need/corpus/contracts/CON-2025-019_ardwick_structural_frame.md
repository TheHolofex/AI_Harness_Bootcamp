# CON-2025-019 - Framework, Selwyn Bridge structural works

**Parties:** Task Group MERIDIAN and Ardwick Structural
**Effective:** 2025-08-01
**Review:** 2027-07-31
**Held by:** Basin Structures Cell
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This framework covers structural works to the Selwyn Bridge at the W-41
crossing, called off by task order.

1.2 The specified works are replacement of the two inner stringers and
replacement of the north pier deck plate, to specification AS-STR-4.

## 2. Certification

2.1 Specification AS-STR-4 may be worked only by a crew holding current AS-STR-4
certification.

2.2 The contractor warrants that it holds one such crew. The contractor gives no
warranty that a second crew will be certified during the term.

2.3 Certification of an additional crew requires eleven weeks from application
and cannot be compressed.

## 3. Programme

3.1 The specified works are programmed at **fourteen weeks**, of which the span
is closed to all traffic for **six weeks**.

3.2 Call-off is subject to crew availability. The contractor will advise
availability on enquiry and is not obliged to release a crew from other
commitments.

## 4. Effect on rating

4.1 On completion and acceptance of the specified works, the structure is
expected to support restoration of the pre-2026 load rating. Restoration is a
matter for the issuing authority and not for the contractor.
