## Ferry Point vehicle crossing

Selwyn Ferry Services has operated the Ferry Point crossing for eleven years. Our
vessel and crew move commercial, relief and official traffic across the Selwyn
seven days a week.

**Throughput: up to 18 vehicles per hour.** Our loading procedure and twin-ramp
slip design mean minimal turnaround between lifts.

**Certified to 30 tonnes per vehicle** under certificate SFS-C-114.

**All-weather operation.** The crossing is engineered for the full range of river
conditions seen in the basin.

Rates on application. Charter and block booking available for sustained
movements. Contact the Ferry Point office.
