## The corridor

The Marrow Cut continues to pool water at the low chainage in heavy rain. It
slows running and has not yet stopped it. The clearance question that was raised
in the spring is settled and does not need revisiting.

The Selwyn Bridge crossing is uneventful. **The bridge takes 40 tonnes and
nothing in our vehicle mix comes close to it**, so the crossing is a matter of
spacing and speed rather than of mass. Serials cross one vehicle at a time at
walking pace and the whole lift is over the span inside twenty minutes.

## Timing

All four serials departed before 0600Z and closed before 1400Z. That is not an
accident of scheduling. **Checkpoint 2 is closed overnight between 2200Z and
0500Z under MEM-2026-041**, so a serial that wants to be through the checkpoint
early has to be timed against the 0500Z opening. In practice that puts departure
from Station Halberd between 0500Z and 0530Z, and everything else follows from
it.

We have run the corridor this way since March and it works. It is worth saying
plainly that it is a manning restriction rather than anything to do with the
route, and if it ever came off, the corridor would open up considerably. That is
not something the Movement Desk can decide.

## Recommendation for September

Route AMBER handled the August lift without difficulty and is the corridor the
desk knows best. On the August evidence it should be the September primary. The
September lift is larger and heavier than anything run in August, so the desk has
asked for the corridor limits to be confirmed rather than assumed.
