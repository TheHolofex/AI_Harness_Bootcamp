## 1. Basis

Times are planning times for a routine load serial of standard vehicles in
daylight, on dry surfacing, with escorts where required already in place at SP.
They are the figures the desk uses to build a programme. They are not the times
a serial will achieve on any given day and they are not a commitment to a
consignee.

Times are quoted head of serial to head of serial. Add the pass time of the
serial where the whole body must be clear of a point.

Format matches the AMBER planning times held by the desk, so that the two can be
compared leg for leg.

## 4. The crossing element

The crossing is not a road leg and is not planned as one. It is a single
allowance covering arrival at the holding area, marshalling, embarkation,
crossing, disembarkation and clearance of the far slip.

a. **Planning allowance: 1h00.** Use this figure when building a programme.

b. The allowance is provisional in every case. It is confirmed only by the slot
   agreed with Selwyn Ferry Services Ltd, and the operator confirms slots the
   evening before the move. Serials released without a confirmed slot will hold
   at Node D.

c. Do not book an onward leg, a consignee reception, an escort handover or a
   crew relief against the planning allowance. Book them against the slot.

d. Where the operator suspends the service because the river is up, no allowance
   applies. The serial turns round at the holding area and returns complete.
   Serials must carry the fuel and rations to do this without refuelling forward
   of Node D.

## 5. Staging

a. **Release point is Node D.** Serials for Ferry Point are held on the Node D
   hardstanding and released against the confirmed slot, not against SP from
   Station Halberd. The desk releases; the detachment commander at Node D does
   not release on his own authority.

b. **One serial forward of Node D at a time.** The approach road and the holding
   area at Ferry Point are single carriageway with limited standing, and a
   second serial arriving behind the first has nowhere to go.

c. **Holding area discipline.** On arrival the serial commander reports to the
   operator's marshal and to the desk. Engines off. Crews remain with vehicles.
   No maintenance or refuelling on the marshalling area.

d. **Far side.** Serials re-form at the far slip clear of the ramp before moving
   off. Torrin Cross is the first point at which a serial can turn round on the
   far side; a serial that has crossed is committed as far as Torrin Cross.

e. **Overnight.** Bastion Reach holds layover accommodation for two crews. A
   serial that cannot complete the return within the crew duty day lays over at
   W-31 and runs on the following morning. This is planned, not exceptional, on
   any task beyond Torrin Cross.

## 6. Cautions

a. **Kellar Ridge.** The descent southbound is on a continuous gradient with two
   tightening bends. Tankers and low loaders use low range throughout. Serials
   close up on the descent and re-establish spacing at the foot.

b. **Ferry Point approach bend.** Poor forward visibility, and civilian traffic
   has been observed overtaking serials on the bend. Escort vehicle to the rear
   where one is tasked.

c. **Torrin Cross.** No overhead clearance restriction, but the junction is
   tight for articulated vehicles and requires a banksman for any vehicle over
   two axles on the trailer.

d. **Surfacing between Node D and Kellar Ridge** is in poor condition over
   roughly 4 km and is on the reactive repairs list. Expect to lose five minutes
   on that stretch in wet weather.
