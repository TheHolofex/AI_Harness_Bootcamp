# Standing Orders - Convoy Spacing and Speed

**Reference:** MOV-2026-083
**From:** Movement Desk, Task Group MERIDIAN, Station Halberd
**To:** All serial commanders; all drivers; all detachments
**Info:** SO2 Operations; Liaison (Marchetti, D.)
**DTG:** 220700ZJUL26
**Effective:** On receipt
**Supersedes:** MOV-2025-092
**Review:** On any change to the road layout on either corridor

**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## 1. General

These orders apply to every Task Group MERIDIAN serial on Routes AMBER and
CINDER, and to accompanied moves run for the Cordell Basin Relief Consortium.
They are minima and maxima. A serial commander may open spacing or reduce speed
at any time and does not require permission to do so. He may not close spacing
below these figures or exceed these speeds on his own authority.

Nothing here overrides a posted civil limit, a works limit, or an instruction
from an escort or a marshal on the ground.

## 2. Spacing

| Condition | Spacing, vehicle to vehicle |
| --- | --- |
| Open road, dry | 100 m |
| Open road, wet or poor visibility | 150 m |
| Built-up and through settlements | 50 m |
| Descending a sustained gradient | 150 m |
| Marrow Cut defile | 50 m, no overtaking, no halting within the defile |
| Kellar Ridge descent (CINDER) | 150 m, low range for tankers and low loaders |
| Approaching a works lane closure | Close to 50 m and follow the marshal |
| Selwyn Bridge span | One vehicle on the span at a time |
| Ferry Point approach and slip | One vehicle on the ramp at a time, under the operator's marshal |
| Halted at a holding area | Nose to tail is not permitted; leave one vehicle length |

## 3. Speed

| Stretch | Maximum |
| --- | --- |
| Open road, both corridors | 60 km/h |
| Open road, wet or poor visibility | 45 km/h |
| Built-up and through settlements | 40 km/h |
| Works lane closures | As posted, and never above 30 km/h |
| Marrow Cut defile | 30 km/h |
| Kellar Ridge descent | 30 km/h |
| Selwyn Bridge span | 15 km/h |
| Ferry Point approach road | 30 km/h |
| Ferry ramp and marshalling area | Walking pace |
| Vehicle parks and hardstanding at all locations | 15 km/h |

Abnormal loads and low loaders run 10 km/h below the figure for the stretch,
and at walking pace over the Selwyn Bridge span.

## 4. Selwyn Bridge (W-41)

a. **One vehicle on the span at a time.** The vehicle ahead must be clear of the
   far abutment before the next enters. This is a spacing rule and applies to
   every vehicle in the serial including escorts.

b. **Speed over the span is 15 km/h**, walking pace for abnormal loads and low
   loaders. No braking, no gear change and no acceleration on the span where it
   can be avoided.

c. **No halting on the span** for any reason short of a breakdown. A vehicle
   that stops on the span is recovered forward off the structure if it can be
   moved at all.

d. **Entry is controlled.** Where a serial of more than four vehicles is
   crossing, the front escort takes station at the near approach and releases
   vehicles one by one. Where no escort is tasked, the serial commander posts a
   man at the near approach and crosses last.

e. **Oncoming traffic has right of way over a serial** at the approach. Do not
   commit a vehicle to the span until the far approach is seen to be clear.

f. **No overtaking on the approaches** within 200 m either side of the
   structure.

## 5. Marrow Cut defile (AMBER)

a. Single direction working. Confirm the defile is clear before the head of the
   serial enters.

b. Close to 50 m and hold it. The defile is not a place to open out.

c. No halting inside the defile. A vehicle that fails inside the defile is towed
   clear before any attempt at repair.

d. Serials of more than four vehicles take a front escort, which enters first
   and reports the defile clear.

## 6. Ferry Point (CINDER)

a. On the approach road and in the marshalling area the operator's marshal has
   control. Serial commanders comply and do not argue on the slip.

b. One vehicle on the ramp at a time, walking pace, no reversing on the ramp
   without a banksman.

c. Engines off in the marshalling area. Crews remain with vehicles. No
   refuelling or maintenance on the marshalling area or the slip.

## 7. Halts and re-forming

a. Planned halt every two hours or 100 km, whichever comes first. Halts are
   taken at nominated points, never on a bend, a bridge approach, a defile or a
   works stretch.

b. On re-forming after a halt, the serial re-establishes full spacing before
   returning to road speed. Do not run closed up to catch the head.

c. A serial that becomes separated does not chase. The rear element proceeds at
   the ordered speed and the head slows or holds at the next safe point.

## 8. Breakdown

a. The rear escort halts with the casualty. The serial continues unless the
   commander judges otherwise.

b. Casualty is moved clear of the running surface where it can be moved.
   Warning signs out front and rear at the distance for the speed of the road.

c. Report to the desk by radio giving location, cause and whether recovery is
   needed. Do not request recovery direct from the workshop.
