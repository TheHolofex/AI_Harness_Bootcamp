## 4. Incidents

a. **20 Feb, W-07 vehicle park.** Reversing cargo vehicle contacted a fence post
   while manoeuvring on to hardstanding. Minor damage to a rear light cluster and
   one post. Insurance file opened under MER/INS/26/007. Driver retained on task.

b. **19 Feb, Marrow Cut, near miss.** Civil contractor plant emerged from a
   works access without a banksman as AMB-26-026 was passing. No contact. Raised
   with the works supervisor the same day and a banksman was posted from 20 Feb.
   Marchetti, D. informed for the Consortium record.

c. **11 Feb, Station Halberd.** Tyre debris in the loading lane, cleared by the
   duty party. No delay to SP.

## 5. Escorts

Escorts were tasked on nine serials, all from Movement Desk resources. Two
requests from the Consortium for accompanied moves on CINDER were met. No escort
task was refused. Escort hours ran under establishment for the month; see the
serviceability return for vehicle availability.
