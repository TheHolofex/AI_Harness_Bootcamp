## 1. Use of this matrix

This matrix is the authority for who may drive what, and where. A driver not
marked for a category does not drive that category, and a driver not marked for
a route does not lead or drive on that route without a route familiarisation run
alongside a marked driver. The desk will not build a serial that the matrix does
not support.

Detachments hold a local extract. The desk copy is the master. Where the two
disagree the desk copy stands.
