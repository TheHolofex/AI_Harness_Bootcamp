## 4. Incidents

a. **04 Jun, AMBER km 14.** Surfacing plant on the low loader fouled overhead
   temporary lighting cable at the works compound entrance. Cable brought down,
   no injury, no damage to the plant. Works electrician attended. Height
   clearance at the compound entrance has since been marked and a banksman is
   required for all plant moves in and out.

b. **11 Jun, W-41.** Minor collision in the vehicle park between a serial
   vehicle and a parked contractor van. Damage to a wing panel. Insurance file
   MER/INS/26/014. Parking layout at W-41 remarked with hatching on 14 Jun.

c. **23 Jun, Station Halberd.** Driver reported feeling unwell before SP and was
   replaced from the relief pool without delay to the serial.

d. **26 Jun, near miss, Ferry Point approach road.** Civilian car overtook the
   rear of a CINDER serial on the approach bend. Reported to the Consortium
   liaison for onward passage to the local authority. Additional warning signage
   requested.

## 5. Escorts

Escorts tasked on eleven serials, two of them abnormal loads requiring escort
front and rear. One serial was deferred for want of an escort at the required
timing (para 3). Escort demand is now the binding constraint on abnormal moves
rather than vehicle or driver availability.

## 6. Remarks

a. Programme pressure in the second and third weeks came from works traffic on
   AMBER running against the Consortium distribution programme on CINDER. Both
   drew on the same escort pool.

b. Slip holds on CINDER were shorter than in May. Evening confirmation of slots
   is now standard practice.

c. The desk continues to refuse demands received after 1600Z on the preceding
   day. Five refusals in June, all met the following day.

d. Driver qualification remains thin in the tanker and abnormal load categories.
   The matrix at MOV-2026-079 refers. Two candidates are booked for training in
   the next quarter.
