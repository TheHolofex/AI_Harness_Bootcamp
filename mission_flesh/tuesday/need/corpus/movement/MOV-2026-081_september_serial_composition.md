## Running assumptions

The lift moves as six serials over the rotation window. The recovery vehicle
runs with the third serial so that it is forward before the bulk of the heavy
cargo moves.

The serial is within the corridor's stated limits and no special crossing
arrangements are anticipated.

## Held for the movement order

Serial timings, escort allocation and the crossing plan are held pending the
route decision.
