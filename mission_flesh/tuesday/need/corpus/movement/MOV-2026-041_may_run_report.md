## 4. Note on CIN-26-068

The serial was released from Node D on a confirmed slot. On arrival at the
marshalling area the ferry master, Ingersoll, P., advised that the operator had
suspended crossings because the river was up, and that no crossings would be
made that day. The desk was informed at 0755Z. The serial was held for two hours
in case the position changed, then returned. Dunphy, M. confirmed the suspension
by telephone at 1015Z and confirmed the resumption at 1740Z the same day.

Crews are reminded that a confirmed slot is not a guarantee of a crossing, and
that a CINDER serial must carry enough fuel and rations to return complete
without refuelling forward of Node D.

   contact. Holding area marshalling brief reissued.

c. **20 May, Station Halberd.** Fuel spill at the fuel point during a bowser
   transfer, contained on the hardstanding by the spill kit. Environmental file
