# MOV-2026-077 - Route AMBER, planning running times

**Issued:** 2026-08-12
**Issuing authority:** Movement Desk, Task Group MERIDIAN
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## Purpose

Planning times for the AMBER corridor, for use in building the September serial
timings. Times are for a thirty vehicle serial in dry conditions.

## Leg times

| Leg | Distance | Planning time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Station Halberd to Checkpoint 2 | 61 km | 1 h 20 | open road, two villages |
| Checkpoint 2, clearance | - | 0 h 45 | serial clearance, one pass |
| Checkpoint 2 to Marrow Cut | 44 km | 1 h 05 | |
| Marrow Cut transit | 6 km | 0 h 50 | single file, ground guide for oversize |
| Marrow Cut to Selwyn Bridge | 39 km | 0 h 55 | |
| Selwyn Bridge crossing | 0.4 km | 0 h 20 | one vehicle on the span at a time |
| Selwyn Bridge to Bastion Reach | 64 km | 1 h 25 | |
| **Total** | **214 km** | **6 h 40** | |

Add 40 minutes in wet conditions, principally at the Marrow Cut.

## The window

**Serials are planned inside the window the checkpoint allows. Checkpoint 2 does
not pass traffic between 2200Z and 0500Z (MEM-2026-041), so the earliest a serial
can clear the checkpoint is 0500Z.** Working back from that, departure from
Station Halberd is 0340Z at the earliest, and in practice the desk times
departure at 0500Z to 0530Z so that the checkpoint clearance is not the first
thing that goes wrong.

With a 6 h 40 running time, a serial departing at 0530Z closes at Bastion Reach
around 1210Z. Departing at 0500Z it closes around 1140Z.

**Route AMBER is planned as a daylight corridor.** Every serial the desk has run
in the current exercise has departed and closed in daylight, and the timings above
assume that.

## Bridge crossing

Twenty minutes is generous for the span itself and is there to absorb the
one-vehicle-at-a-time rule. It assumes no restriction beyond spacing and speed.
If any further restriction applies at the crossing, this figure does not hold and
the timings need rebuilding from the crossing outward rather than from the
checkpoint.

## Note

These are planning times, not a movement order. The September timings will be
built when the route decision is made.
