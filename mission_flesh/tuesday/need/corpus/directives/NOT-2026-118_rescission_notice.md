# NOT-2026-118 - Rescission notice

**Issued:** 2026-06-19
**Issuing authority:** Task Group MERIDIAN, Chief of Staff
**Distribution:** all desks, all detachments, exercise staff
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## Rescission

Checkpoint controller strength at Checkpoint 2 (W-19) was restored on 15 June
2026 with the arrival of the second controller detachment. The condition that
caused the overnight closure no longer holds.

**MEM-2026-041 of 3 March 2026 is rescinded in full with effect from 19 June
2026 0001Z.** The overnight closure of Checkpoint 2 between 2200Z and 0500Z is
lifted. Checkpoint 2 is manned and open around the clock.

**Route AMBER may be planned and run as a night corridor.** Any planning
document that treats AMBER as daylight-only on the authority of MEM-2026-041 is
to that extent out of date.

## Note on onward citation

MEM-2026-041 has been quoted in several planning products since March. This
notice does not amend those products. Planners are responsible for checking
whether a restriction they are relying on is still in force.
