# MEM-2026-041 - Checkpoint 2 overnight closure

**Issued:** 2026-03-03
**Issuing authority:** Movement Desk, Task Group MERIDIAN
**Status line as issued:** in force until rescinded
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## Direction

Following the barrier failure on the night of 27 February and the shortage of
trained checkpoint controllers, Checkpoint 2 (W-19) closes overnight.

**Checkpoint 2 is closed to all movement between 2200Z and 0500Z daily.** No
serial is to be timed through the checkpoint inside that window. Vehicles
arriving inside the window hold at the southern staging area until 0500Z.

This is a manning measure, not a structural one. It will be reviewed when
checkpoint controller strength is restored.

## Effect on Route AMBER

Route AMBER cannot be run as a night corridor while this memorandum stands.
Planners should treat AMBER as a daylight route.
