# ENG-2026-011 - Marrow Cut defile, clearance survey

**Issued:** 2026-04-22
**Issuing authority:** Basin Structures Cell, Task Group MERIDIAN
**Surveyor:** Basin Structures Cell survey team
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## Purpose

The Marrow Cut is the defile on Route AMBER between Checkpoint 2 and the Selwyn Bridge
Bridge. It is the tightest point on the corridor. This survey establishes the
usable clearance.

## Finding

**Usable vertical clearance through the Marrow Cut is 4.1 metres** at the
narrowest section, measured to the rock overhang at chainage 2+140.

Horizontal clearance is 3.4 metres between the rock face and the drainage cut on
the eastern side. Vehicles wider than 3.0 metres should be walked through by a
ground guide.

## Method

Measured by laser range from the carriageway crown at 5 metre intervals through
the narrow section. The 4.1 metre figure is the minimum of 28 readings and
includes no allowance for surface build-up.

## Note on the 2024 figure

An earlier note in circulation gives 3.9 metres. That figure was taken before the
2025 scaling works removed loose rock from the overhang and is superseded by this
survey. Both figures continue to appear in planning products.

## Surface

Surface water pools at chainage 2+060 to 2+110 in heavy rain. Running is slower
but the section stays passable.
