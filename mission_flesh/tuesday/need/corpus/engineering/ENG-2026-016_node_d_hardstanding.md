# ENG-2026-016 - Node D, hardstanding and staging capacity

**Issued:** 2026-06-11
**Issuing authority:** Basin Structures Cell, Task Group MERIDIAN
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## Purpose

Node D (W-07) is the staging point on Route CINDER. This survey establishes what
it can hold and what it would cost to hold more.

## Existing hardstanding

The bound hardstanding at Node D is 4,100 square metres in an L shape around the
fuel point. Marked out for the group's vehicle mix it takes:

| Vehicle type | Bays available |
|---|---|
| Light utility | 22 |
| Medium cargo | 16 |
| Heavy cargo | 9 |
| Oversize (over 3.5 m wide) | 2 |

That is a working capacity of about 47 vehicles staged at once, assuming a
sensible mix and one-way circulation. Push past that and the circulation route is
used for parking, which is where the fuel point gets blocked.

## Unbound area

A further 2,600 square metres to the east is unbound gravel over clay. It is
usable in dry conditions and unusable within a day of heavy rain; the clay comes
up through the gravel and vehicles above about 12 tonnes rut it badly. It has
been used twice and both times required grading afterwards.

Treating this area as staging capacity in a September plan would be optimistic.

## Fuel point

Single fuel point, two hoses, gravity fed from a 30,000 litre bladder farm.
Refuel rate is the binding constraint on turnaround at Node D more often than
space is. A serial of thirty vehicles takes about four hours to put through the
fuel point.

## Circulation

One entrance, one exit, no separation between them for the first 60 metres. A
serial arriving while another is departing waits. This is the single cheapest
thing to fix at Node D and has been on the works list for two years.

## Hire

The hardstanding is held under a hire agreement with the Cordell Basin Relief
Consortium at 2,400 exercise credits per serial staged. The agreement does not
give the group exclusive use, and the consortium has its own traffic through the
site.

## Recommendation

Node D is a workable staging point for a serial at a time. It is not a workable
staging point for a sustained rotation lift without the circulation fix and
without bringing the unbound area up to standard. Neither is programmed.
