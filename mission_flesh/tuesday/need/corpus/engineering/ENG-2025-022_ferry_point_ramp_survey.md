# ENG-2025-022 - Ferry Point, slip and ramp survey

**Issued:** 2025-10-08
**Issuing authority:** Basin Structures Cell, Task Group MERIDIAN
**Surveyor:** Basin Structures Cell survey team
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## Purpose

Condition survey of both slips at the Ferry Point crossing (W-22), carried out at
the request of the Movement Desk during planning for the winter rotation.

## North slip

The north slip is concrete over a rubble base, laid in two campaigns. The upper
apron is sound. The lower apron below the mean water line has spalled at the
edges and the reinforcement is exposed in two places over about 1.2 metres.

Approach gradient is 1 in 9, which is comfortable for the vehicle mix the group
runs. Turning space at the head of the slip is adequate for a single vehicle to
turn, not for two to pass.

## South slip

The south slip is the constraint at this crossing.

The slip is shorter than the north slip and steeper, at 1 in 6.5 over the lower
third. Because it is short, the angle at which the vessel's ramp meets the slip
changes quickly as the river rises. There is a band of river state within which
the ramp bears properly on the slip; outside that band the ramp either grounds
short or bears on its outer edge only.

**The operator holds its own limit for this and works to it.** That limit is a
condition on the vessel's certificate and is a matter for the operator, not for
this cell. We have not been given the figure and have not asked for it; it is not
a structural limit on the slip.

What we can say about the slip itself is that it is sound, that it is short, and
that it will remain sensitive to river state until it is extended. Extension is
not funded.

## Lighting

There is no lighting at either slip. The south slip has no power supply within
400 metres. Any night operation at this crossing would need a generator and mast
set brought in and manned.

## Hardstanding

Vehicle holding at the south side is on unbound gravel and takes about fourteen
vehicles before it fouls the approach. On the north side, holding is on the road
and blocks it.

## Recommendation

Repair the spalled lower apron on the north slip within the year. Extend the
south slip when funds allow. Neither is urgent.
