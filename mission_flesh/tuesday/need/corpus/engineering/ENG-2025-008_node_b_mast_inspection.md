# Annual Structural Inspection - Node B Radio Mast (W-04)

**Reference:** ENG-2025-008
**Asset:** Guyed lattice mast, 30 m, Node B (W-04). Register entry W-04/STR/02
**Inspection date:** 14 March 2025, 0740Z to 1310Z
**Inspected by:** Voss, R. (Estate Works), climbing inspection by Kettleby, J.
**Attending:** Achebe, N. (Signals)
**Previous inspection:** ENG-2024-006, 9 March 2024
**Distribution:** Estate Works file; Signals; Node B detachment

**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## 1. Scope

Annual close inspection of the mast, its guy system, anchor blocks, aviation obstruction lighting, climbing ladder and fall arrest system, and the earthing installation. Feeders, antennas and the equipment cabin are the responsibility of Signals and were not inspected beyond a visual check for fouling against the structure.

The mast was taken off air for the climbing element between 0810Z and 1145Z by arrangement with Achebe, N. Weather was dry with light wind throughout.

## 2. Guy system

Three guy levels at nominal 10 m, 19 m and 27 m, three guys per level at 120 degrees, designated A, B and C by anchor. Tensions were taken at each guy with a calibrated tension meter at 1.5 m above the anchor pin. Design tensions are 5.0 kN at level 1, 4.2 kN at level 2 and 3.4 kN at level 3, tolerance plus or minus ten per cent.

All guys at level 1 and level 3 were within tolerance. Level 2 guy B read 3.5 kN, below tolerance, and was retensioned on site to 4.2 kN with the mast verticality rechecked afterwards by theodolite. Verticality after adjustment was within the permitted deviation over the full height. No strand damage, birdcaging or broken wires were found on any guy. Grips and thimbles are sound.

## 3. Anchors

Three mass concrete anchor blocks. Concrete is in good order with no cracking or spalling visible above ground and no evidence of block movement against the witness marks set in 2023.

Turnbuckle threads at anchor B carry moderate surface corrosion over roughly a third of the exposed length. Locking wire is missing at two turnbuckles at anchor B and one at anchor C. Anchor C stands in a shallow depression which holds water after rain, and the surrounding vegetation has not been cut back this season, obscuring the lower part of the anchor plate.

## 4. Aviation obstruction lighting

Twin medium intensity red obstruction light at the head, photocell controlled. One of the two lamp units was not illuminating on test. The photocell operated correctly on hooding. The cable gland at the head junction box is perished and the sealing compound has shrunk away from the cable. No water ingress was found inside the box at the time of inspection.

## 5. Climbing ladder and fall arrest

Ladder rungs are sound with no measurable loss of section. The fall arrest rail is continuous and correctly aligned; the locking cam on the travelling shuttle shows wear on the engaging face and the shuttle should be replaced at the next service. One cage hoop fixing bolt is missing at approximately 12 m. Cage hoops carry light surface rust from about 18 m upward where the galvanising has weathered.

## 6. Earthing

Earth electrode resistance measured 6 ohms against a maximum permitted 10 ohms. Down conductor clamps are tight. One conductor saddle at 2 m is loose.

## 8. Recommendation

The mast is fit for continued service. Four urgent items are listed above and should be cleared inside two months; the obstruction light unit and the fall arrest shuttle require a further off-air climbing visit and should be combined. Programmed items may be taken with the next annual inspection. No structural repair is required and no change to the inspection interval is proposed.
