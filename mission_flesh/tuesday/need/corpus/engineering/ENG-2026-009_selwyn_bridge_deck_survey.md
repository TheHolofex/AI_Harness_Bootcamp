# ENG-2026-009 - Selwyn Bridge, deck condition survey

**Issued:** 2026-03-30
**Issuing authority:** Basin Structures Cell, Task Group MERIDIAN
**Surveyor:** Jankowski, D., Site Engineer, Ardwick Structural
**Reference:** CON-2025-019
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## Purpose

Follow-up survey to the February load testing, commissioned to establish the
extent of the deck plate work and to price the stringer replacement. This survey
records condition. It does not set or amend a load rating.

## Deck plate, north pier

The plate over the north pier is the governing element. Distortion first noted in
the 2025 annual inspection has progressed. The measured out-of-plane deflection
at the centre of the panel is now 18 mm against 11 mm recorded in March 2025.

The distortion is thermal in origin. The panel is restrained on three edges and
has no relief joint, so it works against itself through the daily cycle. Twelve
years of that cycle have taken the stiffness out of it. The pattern is
consistent, seasonal and predictable, and it is worst in the warmest part of the
afternoon in the summer months.

The panel is not at risk of failure. It is at risk of continuing to lose
stiffness, and the stiffness of that panel is what the rating leans on.

## Inner stringers

Section loss at the two inner stringers is between 9 and 14 per cent of original
web thickness over a 4 metre length either side of the north pier. Loss is by
corrosion at the drainage line, which discharges across the web rather than clear
of it. The drainage fault is the cause and will recur if the stringers are
replaced without also moving the discharge.

## Works required

The specified remedy is replacement of both inner stringers and replacement of
the north pier deck plate, to specification AS-STR-4 under CON-2025-019. Moving
the drainage discharge should be added to the task order; it is a small item and
leaving it out guarantees the problem returns.

**These works are to be scheduled.** Programme and crew availability are matters
for the contractor and are dealt with separately. The Basin Structures Cell has
asked Ardwick Structural for availability and will circulate the answer when it
has it.

## Interim

No interim strengthening is proposed. The structure is being managed by rating
rather than by works, and that is the correct approach while the works are
outstanding. Planners should work from the current rating document and not from
this survey.

## Monitoring

Deflection at the north pier panel to be re-measured at six months. Section loss
at the stringers to be re-measured at twelve months or on completion of the
works, whichever is sooner.
