# Barrier and Search Bay Survey - Checkpoint 2 (W-19)

**Reference:** ENG-2025-031
**Site:** Checkpoint 2 (W-19), Route AMBER
**Survey dates:** 22 and 23 October 2025
**Surveyed by:** Prentice, H. (Estate Works), with Oyelaran, T. (mechanical)
**Raised at request of:** Node commander, W-19, following ENG-2025-029 defect report
**Distribution:** Estate Works file; W-19; Supply (Tan, W.) for spares holding

**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## Purpose

To establish the condition of the vehicle barrier installation, the search bay and its canopy, and the associated surfacing and lighting, and to recommend a course of action on the barrier actuators, which have been the subject of repeated defect reports since the spring.

## Barrier installation

Two hydraulic rising arm barriers, designated B1 (inbound, north face) and B2 (outbound, south face), each with a 4.5 m aluminium boom, local control pedestal and a remote release in the guard post. Both were installed as part of the original fit out and neither has had a major overhaul.

Cycle counters read 41,120 on B1 and 29,640 on B2. The manufacturer's stated service life for the actuator is 30,000 cycles under continuous duty, with a recommended seal set replacement at 15,000. Neither unit has had a seal set replaced.

B1 shows a steady weep at the rod seal, sufficient to leave a stain roughly 300 mm across on the plinth and to require a reservoir top up at each fortnightly service. Boom drift from the raised position was measured at 40 mm over five minutes, against a manufacturer's tolerance of 10 mm. Rise time has lengthened to 7.5 seconds against a specified 4 seconds. B2 is dry at the seals but rise time is 5.5 seconds and the boom bounces perceptibly at the top of travel, indicating loss of damping.

Boom skirts on both barriers are dented and the reflective banding is dirty and partly lifted. The B1 counterweight cover is missing and has been substituted with a plywood panel.

## Search bay

The bay is a 30 m by 8 m bituminous macadam apron with a central inspection strip. Surfacing has failed in the wheel tracks over roughly a third of the bay area, with ravelling, three potholes over 100 mm deep and standing water at the eastern kerb line after rain. The inspection strip drainage channel is blocked with grit along about 6 m of its length. Kerbs are displaced at two points on the northern edge where vehicles have overrun.

Line marking within the bay is worn to the point of being unreadable in the wheel tracks.

## Lighting

Six column mounted heads serve the bay and approaches. Two heads are out. A third flickers on warm up and is judged to be at end of lamp life. Measured illuminance across the bay averaged well below the level specified for a search task, with pronounced dark bands between columns four and five where a head is out. Column bases are sound; one door is missing from column three, leaving the cut out exposed.

## Search bay canopy

Steel portal canopy, six bays, profiled steel sheet roof. Purlins are sound. Sheet fixings have backed out at nine positions along the eastern eaves, and there are two split washers. The gutter is blocked over its full length with leaf litter and is discharging over the edge onto the apron, which accounts for part of the standing water at the eastern kerb. One downpipe is detached at the shoe. Frame paintwork has broken down at the base of three stanchions where salt and grit accumulate, with light section loss on one.

## Recommended actuator replacement programme

Repair of the existing actuators is not recommended. Both units are near or past the stated service life, spares support for the current model is poor, and a seal set on B1 alone would not address the rise time or the damping loss.

The recommendation is a phased replacement of both actuators with a common current model, retaining the existing plinths, booms and control pedestals:

| Stage | Work | Indicative cost, exercise credits |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Replace B1 actuator, hydraulic pack and hoses; recommission | 9,400 |
| 2 | Replace B2 actuator, hydraulic pack and hoses; recommission | 9,400 |
| 3 | Common spares holding: one actuator, two seal sets, hose set | 6,200 |
| 4 | Boom skirt repair and reflective banding renewal, both booms | 1,150 |
| | Total | 26,150 |

Stages 1 and 2 must not be taken concurrently; one lane is to remain in service throughout, with the manual pump raise and a chained boom rest used on the lane out of service.

Surfacing, lighting and canopy items are to be raised as separate works orders. The canopy gutter clearance and the two failed lighting heads are the two items that should not wait for a programme.
