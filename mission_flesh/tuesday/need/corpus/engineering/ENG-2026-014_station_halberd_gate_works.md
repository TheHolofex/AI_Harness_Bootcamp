**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

This instruction authorises the works listed below. It is issued as a single instruction because the items are all at the gate end of the station and are cheaper taken together than as separate visits. There is no single serious defect here; there is a long list of small ones that have been carried forward for two years.

## A. Main gate

A1. Rehang the north leaf. The leaf drops about 25 mm at the free end and fouls the ground on closing. The lower hinge pin is worn oval and is to be replaced; the hinge plate is to be checked for elongation of the bolt holes and shimmed or renewed as required.

A2. Renew the gate stop and the ground socket for both leaves. The existing socket is full of grit and the stop is bent.

A3. Renew the drop bolts, both leaves. The present bolts are seized and have been left permanently withdrawn, which is why the leaves rattle.

A4. Renew the reflective banding on both leaves and the two chevron boards on the gate posts.

A5. Replace the gate luminaire. The unit is corroded through at the gland plate.

A6. Overhaul the pedestrian side gate: new closer, new latch, and free off and grease the hinges.

## B. Guard room

B1. Replace the entrance door and frame. The door is swollen, does not latch reliably in wet weather, and the frame is rotted at the foot of both jambs.

B2. Replace the failed window unit to the observation window. The unit has blown and is permanently misted, which defeats its purpose.

B3. Renew the floor covering throughout, about 14 square metres, with a slip resistant sheet, coved at the edges. The present vinyl is lifting at the doorway and is a trip hazard.

B4. Install two additional double sockets on the rear wall, on a spur from the existing circuit, and remove the extension leads currently in use.

B5. Service the heater and renew the thermostat, which is not controlling.

B6. Redecorate throughout after B1 to B5 are complete.

## C. Perimeter fence

C1. Repair the fence line at the two positions where fallen timber has breached it, at posts 34 to 36 and posts 71 to 73. Temporary wire lashings to be removed. Renew four posts and about 30 m of fabric.

C2. Cut back the tree line to a clearance of 3 m from the fence over the full eastern boundary, and remove the two dead stems that caused the breaches.

C3. Retension the fabric over the northern run, posts 12 to 34, where it is slack enough to be lifted.

C4. Renew the barbed topping over 60 m on the northern run.

C5. Reinstate the fence line inspection path, which is overgrown for most of its length, by strimming and applying a residual herbicide.

## Sequence and access

Work section by section. The main gate is not to be out of use for more than four hours at a time and A1 is to be done in a single shift; the Provost is to be given twenty four hours notice of each gate possession and will run the pedestrian gate and a manual vehicle check in the interim. The guard room is to remain habitable; B1 and B3 are to be done on the same day with a temporary door hung overnight if the new frame is not made good by close of play.

Report completion of each section separately. Do not hold the whole instruction open waiting on the fencing materials.
