# ENG-2026-003 - Selwyn Bridge, structural re-rating

**Issued:** 2026-02-17
**Issuing authority:** Basin Structures Cell, Task Group MERIDIAN
**Signed:** Jankowski, D., Site Engineer (Ardwick Structural), countersigned Basin Structures Cell
**Supersedes:** the load rating carried in ENG-2025-014
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## Finding

The 2025 annual inspection rated the Selwyn Bridge at 40 tonnes gross vehicle
mass. Load testing carried out on 4 and 5 February 2026 does not support that
rating. Section loss at the two inner stringers, and the condition of the deck
plate over the north pier, require the rating to be reduced.

**The Selwyn Bridge is re-rated to 32 tonnes gross vehicle mass with immediate
effect.** The 40 tonne figure in ENG-2025-014 is withdrawn and is not to be
quoted.

## Rating table

| Condition | Gross vehicle mass | Max axle load | Spacing | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 32 t | 11.5 t | one vehicle on span | 25 km/h |
| Convoy running | 28 t | 10.0 t | 40 m between vehicles | 15 km/h |
| Deck temperature above 38 C (note 3) | 24 t | 8.5 t | one vehicle on span | 10 km/h |
| Following a Category 2 flood event | 20 t | 7.0 t | one vehicle on span | 10 km/h |

**Note 1.** Masses are gross laden, including any recovery load under tow.

**Note 2.** Ratings apply to the whole structure. There is no lane-by-lane
variation.

**Note 3.** The deck plate over the north pier loses stiffness as it heats. Where
the measured deck surface temperature exceeds 38 C, the derated row applies for
as long as that condition holds and for one hour after. Deck surface temperature
is measured at sensor SLW-DECK-02 and is not the same as air temperature. Where
no measurement is available, assume the derate applies between 1100Z and 1800Z
from June to September inclusive.

**Note 4.** Restoration to 40 t requires replacement of the two inner stringers
and the north pier deck plate. That work is specified in CON-2025-019 and can
only be carried out by a crew certified to that specification.

## Next inspection

Due 2027-02-17, or immediately after any Category 2 flood event.
