# ENG-2025-014 - Selwyn Bridge, annual inspection

**Issued:** 2025-03-11
**Issuing authority:** Basin Structures Cell, Task Group MERIDIAN
**Inspector:** Basin Structures Cell survey team
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## Summary

The Selwyn Bridge carries Route AMBER over the Selwyn at the W-41 crossing. The
structure was inspected on 3 and 4 March 2025.

**The bridge is rated at 40 tonnes gross vehicle mass.** No restriction is placed
on convoy running. Speed over the span is limited to 25 km/h.

## Condition

The deck surface is sound. Minor section loss was noted at the inner stringers
and is within tolerance for the current rating. The north pier deck plate shows
early heat distortion in summer conditions; this is recorded for monitoring and
does not affect the rating at this inspection.

Approach surfacing on the north side is worn and should be re-laid within two
years. Drainage at both abutments is clear.

## Recommendation

Re-inspect at twelve months. Bring forward if section loss at the inner stringers
progresses, which would put the 40 tonne rating in question.
