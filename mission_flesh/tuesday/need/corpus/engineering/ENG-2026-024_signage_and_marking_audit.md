# Signage and Road Marking Audit - Routes AMBER and CINDER

**Reference:** ENG-2026-024
**Audit dates:** 2 and 3 June 2026
**Audited by:** Nkemdirim, A. (Estate Works)
**Extent:** Route AMBER, 0+000 to 27+500. Route CINDER, 0+000 to 22+100
**Method:** Daylight drive-through in both directions at posted speed, with a walked check at each defect
**Distribution:** Estate Works file; Movement Desk (Iyer, S.)

**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

Both corridors were driven in both directions. Every permanent sign on the estate register was located except two, noted below. Sign faces were assessed for legibility, retroreflectivity by night-drive comparison against a new plate carried in the vehicle, cleanliness, alignment and mounting condition. Road markings were assessed for wear, and where worn, for whether the marking could still be followed at night in rain.

Sixty one permanent signs and roughly 41 km of marking were audited. Forty seven signs were found satisfactory and required no action. Defects are listed below.

Priority: 1, replace or repair within one month. 2, within the current quarter. 3, next planned maintenance visit.
