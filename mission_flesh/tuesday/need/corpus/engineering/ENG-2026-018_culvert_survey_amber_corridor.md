# Culvert Survey - Route AMBER Corridor

**Reference:** ENG-2026-018
**Survey dates:** 13 to 16 April 2026
**Surveyed by:** Oyelaran, T. and Nkemdirim, A. (Estate Works)
**Extent:** Route AMBER, chainage 0+000 (Station Halberd north gate) to 27+500 (south approach, Selwyn Bridge)
**Method:** Walked survey with pole camera at all culverts; man entry at AMB-C08 and AMB-C13 only
**Distribution:** Estate Works file; Movement Desk (Iyer, S.); node commanders W-19

**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## 1. Scope and method

Fourteen culverts are recorded on the corridor between the station north gate and the south approach to the Selwyn Bridge. All fourteen were located and surveyed. Two had not been recorded correctly in the register: AMB-C05 is listed as 450 mm and is in fact 600 mm, and AMB-C12 is listed at 21+700 and is at 21+770. The register is to be corrected.

Inverts and outfalls were inspected from both ends. Where the culvert was too small for entry a pole camera was used and the record consists of stills at the inlet, the outlet and, where the run could be seen through, the midpoint. Two culverts, AMB-C08 and AMB-C13, are of a size that permits entry and were walked through with a confined space team.

Condition is graded 1 to 5 on the standing scale: 1 clear and sound; 2 minor silting or minor defects; 3 significant silting or defects, function reduced; 4 function substantially lost; 5 collapsed or blocked.

Flows were low at the time of survey. Nothing in this report should be taken as an assessment of hydraulic capacity, which has not been calculated for any of these structures. The survey establishes physical condition only.

## 4. Recommendations

4.1 **AMB-C11 at 19+050** is the only structure on the corridor whose condition is a genuine concern. The corrugated steel invert has perforated and the crown is distorting, which is the sequence that precedes a collapse under load. It is to be relined or replaced this financial year. Pending that work it should be added to the fortnightly patrol and inspected after any heavy rain, and the road above it should be checked for depression at the same visit.

4.2 **AMB-C03 at 4+710** is functionally blocked and should be cleared as soon as a jetting unit is available. It is a small pipe in an area of low flow and is not urgent in the same sense as C11, but the ponding upstream is beginning to soften the verge.

4.3 Nine culverts require jetting or vegetation clearance. These should be issued as one instruction to a single jetting contractor working the corridor north to south, which is far cheaper than calling a unit out to each in turn. Estimated cost of the clearance instruction, 18,400 exercise credits.

4.4 The outlet apron at AMB-C05 and the cracked headwall at AMB-C09 are small masonry jobs and can be taken by the site works team.

4.5 The corridor is worked in daylight, Checkpoint 2 passing no traffic between 2200Z and 0500Z under MEM-2026-041, so all of this work will be done under traffic. Short stop-go working at each culvert is sufficient; none of these items needs a closure.

4.6 Next survey due April 2027. AMB-C04, AMB-C08 and AMB-C13 carry specific items to be re-examined and the surveyor is to be given this report before going out.
