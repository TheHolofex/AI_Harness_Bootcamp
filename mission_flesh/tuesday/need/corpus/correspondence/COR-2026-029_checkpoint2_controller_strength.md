# Memorandum: Checkpoint Controller Strength, Checkpoint 2 (W-19)

Reference: COR-2026-029
Issued: 24 March 2026, 1150Z
From: Personnel and Manning Cell, Task Group MERIDIAN
To: Chief of Staff, Task Group MERIDIAN
Copy to: Iyer, S. (Movement Desk); Detachment Commander, Checkpoint 2 (W-19); Achebe, N. (Signals); Marchetti, D. (Liaison)
Filed by: Headquarters registry, MERIDIAN

**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

1. **Purpose.** To record the checkpoint controller position at Checkpoint 2 and to
set out, for the file, the manning reasoning behind the overnight closure now in
force under MEM-2026-041.

2. **The closure.** MEM-2026-041 directs that Checkpoint 2 passes no traffic
between 2200Z and 0500Z. Route AMBER is accordingly worked as a daylight
corridor. The direction took effect this month and stands as issued.

3. **Why manning drove it.** A checkpoint controller post at Checkpoint 2 is a
qualified post. Controllers are the only people at the checkpoint authorised to
clear a vehicle through the barrier, to hold a vehicle for documentation, and to
sign the movement log. Gate hands, drivers and stores staff cannot substitute for
them and the standing orders do not permit an unqualified stand-in.

4. **Strength.** The checkpoint is established for six controllers, which supports
three shifts with a relief. It currently holds four.

| Controller | Status |
|---|---|
| Controller 1 | Held. Substantive. |
| Controller 2 | Held. Substantive. |
| Controller 3 | Held. Substantive. |
| Controller 4 | Held. On temporary attachment from Node D, due back to parent unit. |
| Controller 5 | Vacant since 4 February 2026. |
| Controller 6 | Vacant. Never filled on establishment. |

5. **Consequence of four.** Four controllers cover two shifts with no relief and
no margin for leave, sickness or training. There is no third shift to be had. The
attempt to cover a twenty-four-hour period with four qualified controllers in
February produced the following, all recorded in the checkpoint occurrence book:

- Two occasions on which the barrier was worked by a single controller with no
  second person on the documentation desk, contrary to standing orders.
- One occasion on which the checkpoint stood down between 0100Z and 0330Z with no
  controller present and no notice to the Movement Desk, because the controller on
  watch was taken ill and there was nobody to call in.
- A sustained failure to complete the night movement log to standard, which
  Movement Desk raised separately.

6. **The choice.** Holding four controllers, the checkpoint can be manned properly
for part of the day or improperly for all of it. The direction at MEM-2026-041
takes the first. Between 0500Z and 2200Z the checkpoint is manned to standard,
with two qualified controllers on shift and the documentation desk covered.
Between 2200Z and 0500Z it passes no traffic and the controllers are off watch.

7. **What this is not.** This is not a security measure, a traffic management
measure or a road condition measure. Movement staff planning against AMBER should
treat the overnight period as closed for the reason stated and plan daylight
movement accordingly. Queries about the closure should come to this cell rather
than to the checkpoint, whose staff have had a good deal of it already.

8. **Recruitment and qualification.** The two vacant posts are with the manning
authority. Controller 4's attachment is a loan and the parent unit at Node D has
asked twice for the individual back. This cell has no authority to extend the
attachment unilaterally and has said so.

9. **Recommendation.** That the Chief of Staff notes the position, and that the
manning authority be pressed at formation level for the two vacancies. Until the
checkpoint holds six qualified controllers it cannot be manned to standard around
the clock, and MEM-2026-041 stands.
