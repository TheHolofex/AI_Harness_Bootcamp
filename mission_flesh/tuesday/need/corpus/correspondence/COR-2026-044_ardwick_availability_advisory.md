Responding to your enquiry of 29 April about scheduling the stringer and deck
plate replacement.

We hold the only crew certified to the specification in CON-2025-019. That crew
is committed to the Corran Narrows lock rebuild and will not be released before
**30 September 2026**. The lock authority has a liquidated damages clause we are
not going to breach for a scheduling favour.

To be plain about what that means for you: we cannot start the Selwyn Bridge work
before the beginning of October, and the work itself is fourteen weeks with the
span closed for six of them. Nothing we do restores the 40 tonne rating inside
this year's autumn rotation window.

We have looked at whether a second crew could be certified in time. It cannot;
certification alone is eleven weeks and we would then be training against a
specification that assumes crew experience we would not have.

If your planning depends on the higher rating being back before October, it needs
to stop depending on that now rather than in September.
