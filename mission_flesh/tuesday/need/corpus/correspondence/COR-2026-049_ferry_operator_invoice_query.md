## Part 1: Outgoing query

Sent: 15 June 2026, 1105Z
From: Finance Cell, Task Group MERIDIAN (Okonkwo, B., signing)
To: Accounts, Selwyn Ferry Services Ltd
Copy to: Dunphy, M. (Operations Manager); Iyer, S. (Movement Desk)

Dear Sirs,

Invoice SFS/26/0511, covering the period 1 to 31 May 2026, is held pending
clarification of two lines. Payment of the balance of the invoice is not in
dispute and will be released on the normal terms.

**Query 1: the monthly minimum.** The invoice charges the monthly minimum in full.
Our understanding of clause 6 of the carriage agreement is that the minimum is a
floor on our commitment for a month in which the service was available throughout,
and that where the operator lifts the service the minimum abates for the days on
which no service was offered. The service was lifted on a number of days in May.
The invoice does not appear to reflect any abatement.

**Query 2: presentation of lift days.** The invoice shows a single line for the
month with no breakdown. We cannot reconcile it against our own movement records
without knowing which days the operator treats as lift days and which it treats as
days of service. Where a lift was called part way through an operating day, we do
not know how the operator has treated that day.

We would be grateful for a schedule of lift days for the month and a note of how
clause 6 has been applied. If our reading of clause 6 is wrong we would rather be
told plainly than continue to raise this every month, which is what has been
happening since March.

Yours faithfully,

B. Okonkwo
Finance Cell
Task Group MERIDIAN

## Part 2: Operator's reply

Sent: 19 June 2026, 1440Z
Received: 19 June 2026, 1502Z
From: Dunphy, M., Operations Manager, Selwyn Ferry Services Ltd
To: Finance Cell, Task Group MERIDIAN
Copy to: Accounts, Selwyn Ferry Services Ltd

Dear Mr Okonkwo,

Thank you for your letter of 15 June. I have taken this myself rather than leaving
it with Accounts, because the point is one of interpretation rather than
arithmetic and it will save us both time.

**On the monthly minimum.** Your reading of clause 6 is not the one we work to,
and I think the difference is worth setting out properly.

The minimum is not a payment for a volume of carriage. It is a payment for holding
the service available to you. We keep a crew rostered, a master on watch, a marshal
at the slipway and a vessel maintained and crewed against your requirement, whether
or not you present a vehicle and whether or not the river permits us to sail on a
given day. Those costs do not abate when the master lifts the service. The crew is
still paid, the vessel is still maintained, and we still turn out the following
morning.

Clause 6 provides for abatement in one case only: where the vessel is out of
service for a cause within our control, which in practice means our own maintenance
overruns beyond the published maintenance days. It does not abate for a lift called
by the master. A lift called on grounds of river state, visibility or slipway
safety is expressly outside the abatement provision. Clause 11 puts it beyond doubt
by excluding our liability for delay however caused, and we would say the same
logic runs through clause 6.

I appreciate this is not the answer you want. It is the answer we have given to
every customer on the same terms and I am not able to give you a different one.

**On presentation.** Here I agree with you entirely and we should have done better.
A single line for the month is not good enough for a customer who has to reconcile
it. From the July invoice we will show, as a schedule to the invoice:

- each day of the month, marked as a day of service, a lift day, or a published
  maintenance day;
- for a lift called during an operating day, the time the lift was called and the
  time the service resumed, if it did;
- the standing charge, the minimum, and any charges above the minimum, shown
  separately.

I have asked Accounts to reissue SFS/26/0511 in that form so you have a like-for-
like starting point. The reissued invoice will be for the same total. The value
does not change; only the presentation does.

**On the recurring nature of this.** You are right that we have exchanged letters
on this every month since March. If the Task Group wishes to revisit clause 6 at
the next renewal that is a proper conversation to have with our commercial
director, and I would support having it. In the meantime the clause reads as it
reads and our invoices will continue to follow it.

Yours sincerely,

M. Dunphy
Operations Manager
Selwyn Ferry Services Ltd

**Finance Cell minute, 22 June 2026.** Reissued invoice received and matches. The
