Further to your request for our operating limits at Ferry Point, in writing as
asked.

**MV Tolland Carrier does not operate when the Selwyn gauge at the Ferry Point
reach exceeds 3.20 metres.** That is an insurance condition on the hull and we
have no discretion over it. Above that gauge the ramp angle at the south slip
puts the vessel outside her certificate and we suspend until the gauge falls and
holds below 3.20 for six hours.

We take the gauge from the Basin Hydrology Cell series, same as you do. We do not
run our own gauge.

Two further points you should have in writing:

The vessel is certified to 18 tonnes per vehicle. I am aware our brochure quotes
a higher figure against certificate SFS-C-114. That certificate is for MV Corran
Trader, which we sold in 2024. Tolland Carrier holds SFS-C-131. The brochure has
not been reprinted and I would not rely on it.

We work daylight only at Ferry Point. There is no lighting on the south slip and
we have not been funded to install any.

I am happy to put any of this in front of your planners.
