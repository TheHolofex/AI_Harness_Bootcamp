Dear Ms Iyer,

We are changing our published sailing pattern and our shore office hours at Ferry
Point with effect from Monday 2 March 2026. Please substitute the pattern below
for the one issued in September 2025 and withdraw the earlier card from your
movement boards.

## New pattern

| Day | First sailing | Last sailing | Break |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | 0530Z | 2015Z | 1145Z to 1230Z |
| Saturday | 0600Z | 1845Z | 1200Z to 1245Z |
| Sunday | 0700Z | 1730Z | None |

The midday break is new on weekdays and Saturdays. It exists so that the crew can
take a proper meal without the vessel standing idle at odd times through the
afternoon, which is what happened under the old arrangement. Crew changeover
moves from 1300Z to 1215Z on weekdays and will normally be complete inside the
break.

Sailings run on demand within the operating window rather than to fixed times, as
before. The slipway marshal will hold vehicles at the top of the ramp when the
vessel is loading and will not accept a vehicle onto the ramp once the ramp gate
is set.

## Shore office hours

The Ferry Point shore office will be staffed 0500Z to 1600Z Monday to Friday and
0600Z to 1200Z on Saturdays. It will be closed on Sundays. Outside those hours
the office telephone diverts to the duty number, which is answered by the master
on watch. Please do not use the duty number for booking enquiries, invoice
queries or bulk movement planning. Those should come to the shore office in
working hours and will be dealt with in the order received.

Bookings for the following day now close at 1500Z rather than at 1700Z. This is
the change most likely to affect your desk. We have brought it forward because
the late bookings were arriving after the marshal had gone off shift and were
being reconstructed from telephone notes the next morning, with the errors you
would expect.

## Suspension of service

Our standing position on suspension is unchanged. The master suspends the service
at his own judgement when the river is up, when visibility on the crossing is
poor, or when the slipway on either bank is unsafe to work. Notice of suspension
goes out on the duty number and by hand to the marshal's board at both slipways.
We do not undertake to give advance warning of a suspension and we do not run a
reduced service during one. When the service is suspended it is suspended
entirely.

Vehicles held at the slipway when a suspension is called will be turned back to
the marshalling area. There is no standing space on the approach and we ask that
your movement staff do not queue vehicles on the approach road in any
circumstances, suspension or not.

## Maintenance days

MV Tolland Carrier comes off service for routine maintenance on the second
Wednesday of each month. On those days the service does not run at all. The dates
for the next quarter are 11 March, 8 April and 13 May 2026. If a maintenance day
has to move we will give at least seven days' notice through the shore office.

## Other points

- The vehicle weighbridge at the north slipway remains out of service. Weights
  are taken from the movement documentation presented at the ramp.
- The waiting shelter at the south slipway has been re-roofed and is back in use.
- We have repainted the ramp edge markings on both banks. The paint is slippery
  when wet until it has weathered and we have put out cones and a warning sign.
