**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

1. This note explains, in plain terms, who carries what liability when our vehicles
and people use Consortium roads, hardstanding and vehicle parks. It replaces the
one-page summary circulated in 2025, which several people found unclear.

2. **The basic split.** The Consortium insures the ground. We insure what we bring
onto it and what we do there. So:

| Situation | Carried by |
|---|---|
| Injury to a third party caused by our vehicle or our people | Task Group |
| Damage to Consortium fixed assets (kerbs, gullies, columns, fencing, gates) caused by us | Task Group, recharged at cost |
| Injury caused by a defect in the Consortium's surface, lighting or drainage | Consortium |
| Damage to our vehicles caused by a defect in the Consortium's surface | Consortium, subject to notice |
| Damage to our vehicles caused by our own driving | Task Group |
| Loss or theft from a vehicle or container on hired hardstanding | Task Group |
| Pollution from our fuelling or servicing | Task Group |
| Pollution from Consortium plant or from the interceptor itself | Consortium |

3. **Notice is the whole of it.** The Consortium's cover for surface and lighting
defects operates only where the defect was reported and not made good within a
reasonable period, or where it was not reasonably discoverable. If a pothole opens
on Consortium hardstanding, is not reported by anybody, and three weeks later a
vehicle drops a wheel into it, we will not recover. Report defects. The reporting
route is the Consortium lodge at each site, in writing, with a copy to this cell.
A telephone call alone is not a report.

4. **Our people are to complete an incident form for every contact, however
trivial.** A scraped mirror on a gate post is a contact. The Consortium's own
practice is the same and their drivers are diligent about it. The two occasions
last year when we could not resolve a dispute both turned on the absence of a form
written on the day.

5. **Cover in force.** The Task Group holds third-party liability cover to the
limit set out in the standing financial instructions, and the Consortium holds
public liability cover which it evidences annually. Their current certificate was
produced to the Finance Cell on 11 May 2026 and is on file. Ours goes to them in
September on the same cycle.

6. **Contractors.** Ardwick Structural and any other contractor on site carry their
own cover and are not covered by either of the arrangements above. Where a
contractor's works occupy part of a shared road or hardstanding, the occupied area
passes to the contractor for the period of occupation and comes back on handover.
Handovers are to be recorded in writing at both ends. This matters at the Marrow
Cut, where the drainage scheme will take road space for some weeks, and at Ferry
Point, where the slipway edge protection works will occupy part of the marshalling
area when they are instructed.

7. **The ferry.** Movement on and off MV Tolland Carrier, and everything on the
vessel and on the ramp, sits under the ferry operator's own conditions of carriage,
not under this note. Those conditions are in the file with the ferry agreement. The
short version is that the operator's liability for a vehicle begins at the ramp
gate and ends at the ramp gate on the far bank, and that the operator excludes
liability for delay of any kind, including delay caused by suspension of the
service. Our own cover picks up the vehicle either side of that.

8. **Bridges and structures.** Where our vehicles use the Selwyn Bridge, use is
subject to the structure's posted 40 tonne rating and the normal rules for
crossings. Compliance with the posting is a driver and movement staff
responsibility. No insurance arrangement of any kind responds to a loss arising
from an overloaded crossing, and none of the cover described above would.

9. **Claims handling.** All claims, ours and against us, go through the Finance
Cell. Detachments are not to admit liability, agree a figure, or arrange a repair
with a third party directly. This has happened twice and has cost us more than the
original damage on both occasions.

10. **Summary for the noticeboard.** Report defects in writing. Fill in the form.
Do not admit liability. Do not settle anything yourself.
