Following the quarterly services meeting on 1 April, the schedules for waste
removal and water supply at shared sites are confirmed as below. There are two
changes from the previous schedule, both minor, and both marked.

| Node D (W-07) | General | 1100 litre wheeled bin x 4 | Tuesday and Friday | None |
| Node D (W-07) | Recyclate, mixed dry | 1100 litre wheeled bin x 2 | Friday | None |

| Marrow Cut compound | General | Skip, 8 cubic yard | Fortnightly, even weeks | None |
| Marrow Cut compound | Inert spoil | Skip, 12 cubic yard | On call | New |

Collections are made between 0700Z and 1100Z. Bins are to be presented at the
marked collection points and not at the container compound gate, which the vehicle
cannot reach when the compound is occupied. This has been said before.

On-call collections require twenty-four hours' notice to the Consortium site
services desk. Requests made after 1400Z will be treated as received the following
working day.

The general waste bins at Node D are being contaminated with cardboard and
shrink-wrap. The recyclate bins next to them are frequently empty. Where a general
bin is presented with obvious recyclate in it the Consortium reserves the right to
apply the contamination charge in the schedule at paragraph 3.

## 2. Water supply

| Site | Supply | Arrangement | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Node D (W-07) | Mains, metered | Meter read monthly, first working day | None |
| Node D (W-07) | Vehicle washing standpipe | Metered separately, key from lodge | None |
| Ferry Point (W-22) | Mains, metered | Meter read monthly, first working day | None |
| Bastion Reach (W-31) | Bowser, delivered | Two fills per week, Monday and Thursday | None |
| Marrow Cut compound | Bowser, delivered | One fill per week, Monday | None |

Bowser fills are of potable water and the Consortium samples quarterly. The last
sample round was on 12 March and all results were satisfactory. Certificates are
held at the Consortium lodge and copies are available on request.

The standpipe at Node D was left running overnight on 27 March and again on 2
April. The consumption shows plainly on the meter. Please remind users that the
standpipe key is to be returned to the lodge and the valve closed. A third
occurrence will result in the standpipe being locked out of hours.
