**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

1. This is the quarterly welfare return for Bastion Reach, plus three requests.
The detachment is in reasonable order and morale is fair. Most of what follows is
routine. Paragraphs 6, 9 and 12 need action.

## Accommodation

2. The detachment lives in six accommodation modules on the eastern lines, plus
the converted stores building used as the junior ranks' rest room. Occupancy is
comfortable at present. Two modules have a spare bed space each and I am holding
those against reinforcement rather than spreading people out.

3. Heating in modules 3 and 5 has been intermittent since February. The heaters
run but cut out in the small hours and have to be reset at the panel. Ardwick
looked at it in March and put it down to the control board rather than the heaters
themselves. Nothing further has happened. It is now warm enough that this has
stopped being urgent, which is exactly why it will still be broken in October if
somebody does not chase it.

4. The surface water drainage works to the accommodation lines (Ardwick order
WO-4426) are on site and have made a real difference to the path between the
modules and the ablutions block, which used to stand in water after any
substantial rain. Their people have been tidy and have kept the lines open.

5. The ablutions block is adequate. Six showers, four working reliably. The two at
the far end lose pressure whenever the vehicle washing point is in use. This is a
known feature of the plumbing and I am not asking for it to be redesigned, but a
notice asking people not to run the washing point during the morning shower period
would help and I have put one up.

6. **Request one.** Blackout curtains for modules 1, 2 and 6. The modules face
east, the nights are getting shorter, and the night shift cannot sleep. The
curtains in the other three modules were fitted last year and work well. Cost
should be modest against the welfare account. I have raised a demand and would
like it supported.

## Catering

7. Catering is delivered from the central kitchen at Station Halberd with a
regeneration point here. Three meals are provided. Breakfast 0500Z to 0700Z, main
meal 1130Z to 1300Z, evening meal 1730Z to 1900Z. A night meal is left in the hot
cabinet for the watch and is signed for.

8. Quality is variable in the way that regenerated food always is. The detachment
grumbles about it and eats it. Two specific points:

- The night meal is frequently the same as the evening meal, which by the time the
  night watch eats it has been in the cabinet for some hours. Sandwiches and soup
  would be preferred by most of the detachment to a fourth reheat of the main dish.
- Fresh fruit arrives twice a week and is gone within a day. A third delivery
  would be used.

9. **Request two.** That the Catering Warrant Officer consider a separate night
meal specification for the detachments rather than a portion of the evening meal
held over. I recognise this is a change to the ration cycle and may not be simple.

10. The regeneration point itself is serviceable. The second oven has a failed
door seal and has been on the works list since January. The kitchen hand is working
around it. It has not yet caused a food safety issue and I would rather it was
fixed before it does.

## Welfare and recreation

11. The rest room has a television, a kettle, a small library of paperbacks left by
successive detachments, and a table tennis table with one net. There is a limited
amount of enthusiasm for table tennis. Internet access for personal use is
available at two terminals in the rest room and is heavily used in the evenings,
with a queue most nights.

12. **Request three.** Two further terminals for the rest room. This is the single
thing the detachment asks for most often and by a wide margin. The Signals cell
has confirmed the bandwidth is there. It is a matter of the terminals and a bench.

13. Padre visits have been monthly and are valued. The last was on 14 April. The
next is due in the week beginning 11 May.

14. Medical cover is by the detachment orderly with the Aid Station at Station
Halberd for anything beyond first aid. Mrs Halloran's staff visited on 2 April and
inspected the treatment room. Their observations were minor and have been actioned.
The nearest civil hospital is at Cordell and the evacuation route is by road via
Node D. This was rehearsed on 17 March and took rather longer than the plan
assumes, largely because of the state of the track between the lines and the main
road. That track is not on anybody's works list and probably should be.

15. Sports and adventurous training: nil this quarter. Nobody has had the time.

16. Disciplinary matters: nil. Occurrence book entries for the quarter: eleven, of
which nine are vehicle movements after hours, one a minor cut requiring a dressing,
and one a report of a fox in the stores compound.

Detachment Commander
Bastion Reach (W-31)
