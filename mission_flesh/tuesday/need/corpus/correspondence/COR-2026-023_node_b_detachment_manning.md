3. The deputy's post has been vacant since 11 January. The clerk's post has been
vacant since the turn of the year and I have absorbed the work across the
watchkeepers, which is the direct cause of most of what follows. Two of the four
gate and barrier posts were never filled on arrival. The signaller shortfall is
the one I care about most, because a single signaller cannot cover a
twenty-four-hour watch and I have been borrowing from the watchkeepers to do it.

## 4. Current pattern

The detachment works two twelve-hour shifts, 0600Z to 1800Z and 1800Z to 0600Z,
with a nominal one-in-four rest day. In practice the rest day has been taken by
about half the detachment in the last four weeks and by none of the watchkeepers.
Three people are carrying leave they cannot take. I have stopped issuing rest days
against the roster because it was becoming a fiction.

## 5. Effects

5.1 Vehicle clearance at the main barrier is slower than it should be at the morning
peak, because I am running one person on a two-person post between 0600Z and
0900Z. Drivers wait. Nobody has complained formally but they have complained.

5.2 The stores counter is now open 0800Z to 1200Z and 1300Z to 1600Z only. It was
open through the day. Mr Tan's staff have been told.

5.3 Routine vehicle checks on the internal road are not being done to the
frequency in the standing orders. I have recorded this in the detachment occurrence
book rather than quietly letting the frequency slip.

5.4 The night watch is being kept by one watchkeeper and one gate hand for most of
the week. That is thin. It is adequate for a quiet night and it is not adequate for
anything else.

## 6. Options

6.1 **Option A.** Reinforce by four: deputy, clerk, one signaller, one gate hand.
This restores the pattern with rest days and needs no other change. It is my
preference and I recognise it is the least likely.

6.2 **Option B.** Reinforce by two (signaller and clerk) and accept a permanently
reduced stores counter and a single-person morning barrier. Workable. Not
comfortable.

6.3 **Option C.** No reinforcement, and I move to a three-watch pattern of eight
hours with a shorter working day at the barrier, closing the second lane between
1900Z and 0600Z. This is the option that changes what Node B can do rather than how
tired the detachment is. I would want it recorded that I proposed it as a way of
staying inside a safe working pattern and not as an efficiency.

7. I am not asking for a review of establishment. The establishment is right for
the task. I am asking to be filled to it, or to be told which of the above I am to
adopt.

8. For completeness, and because it comes up whenever anyone plans against Node B:
Checkpoint 2 passes no traffic between 2200Z and 0500Z under MEM-2026-041, so
Route AMBER is worked as a daylight corridor. That does not relieve Node B, which
sits on CINDER and carries traffic at all hours. If anything it has pushed more
of the night work onto us.

9. Request a decision by 20 March 2026.
