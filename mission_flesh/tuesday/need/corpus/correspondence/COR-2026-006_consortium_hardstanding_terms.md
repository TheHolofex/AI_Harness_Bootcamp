Dear Colleagues,

This letter sets out the Consortium's terms for the hire of hardstanding, vehicle
parking and ancillary ground at Node D for the period 1 February 2026 to 31
January 2027. It supersedes the terms circulated in January 2025 and the interim
variation agreed by exchange of signals in August 2025.

## 1. Areas offered

The Consortium offers four discrete parcels at Node D. Areas are as marked on the
site plan at Annex A (unchanged from last year save for the eastern boundary of
Parcel 3, which has moved to follow the new fence line).

| Parcel | Description | Surface | Nominal area | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Main vehicle park, north of the weighbridge house | Reinforced concrete, joints resealed 2025 | 4,100 sq m | Lit. Drainage to interceptor. |
| 2 | Overflow standing, west apron | Bound stone, patched | 2,600 sq m | Unlit. Soft at the south-west corner after prolonged rain. |
| 3 | Container and stores compound | Concrete slab with gravel margins | 1,450 sq m | Fenced and gated. Gate key held at the Consortium lodge. |
| 4 | Turning head and marshalling lane | Concrete | 900 sq m | No standing permitted. Manoeuvring only. |

Parcels are offered whole. The Consortium will not sublet part of a parcel to a
third party while it is on hire to the Task Group.

## 2. Charges

Charges are quoted in exercise credits and are payable monthly in arrears against
a certified return of occupied area. Rates are held flat for the year.

| Item | Basis | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Parcel 1 | Per sq m per month | 0.42 credits |
| Parcel 2 | Per sq m per month | 0.28 credits |
| Parcel 3 | Per sq m per month | 0.51 credits |
| Parcel 4 | Fixed monthly charge | 395 credits |
| Perimeter lighting (Parcels 1 and 3) | Fixed monthly charge | 240 credits |
| Interceptor servicing recharge | Per visit, at cost plus 8 per cent | As invoiced |
| Out-of-hours gate attendance | Per hour, minimum three hours | 65 credits |

Standing charges run from the first of the month in which occupation begins. Part
months are not apportioned. This is a change from last year's terms and was the
principal point of discussion at the December liaison meeting.

## 3. Conditions of use

3.1 The hirer is responsible for keeping parcels free of loose debris, spilled
aggregate and discarded packaging. The Consortium will recharge sweeping if it has
to arrange it.

3.2 No fuelling, oil changes or wet servicing on Parcels 2 or 4. Fuelling is
permitted on Parcel 1 at the marked point only, where the drainage runs to the
interceptor.

3.3 Vehicles are to be parked square to the marked bays on Parcel 1. Continued
oblique parking across bay lines was raised twice in 2025 and remains a source of
complaint from the Consortium's own drivers, who cannot get their trailers round
the northern kerb line when it happens.

3.4 The marshalling lane on Parcel 4 is to be kept clear at all times. It is the
only route by which the Consortium's own tankers reach the water point.

3.5 Any damage to kerbs, gullies, fencing, lighting columns or gate furniture is
to be reported to the Consortium lodge within twenty-four hours. Repairs will be
carried out by the Consortium and recharged at cost.

3.6 The hirer is to hold third-party liability cover in respect of the hired
areas and to produce evidence of it on request. Our separate correspondence on
liability arrangements for shared roads and hardstanding applies without change.

## 4. Notice and variation

Either party may vary the parcels on hire by fourteen days' written notice.
Termination of the whole agreement requires sixty days' notice from either side.
The Consortium reserves access to all parcels at reasonable notice for statutory
inspection of drainage and lighting, and without notice in an emergency.

## 5. Points carried forward from 2025

- The gravel margins on Parcel 3 continue to shed material into the gully at the
  compound gate. The Consortium has programmed a kerb upstand but has not yet
  placed the order.
- Two lighting columns on the northern run of Parcel 1 are on a photocell that
  has been unreliable since the autumn. The Consortium's electrical contractor
  has been asked to attend.
- The weighbridge house window remains boarded following the incident in
  November. Glazing is on order.

Please confirm acceptance in writing by 31 January 2026, or advise which parcels
you wish to take and which you wish to release. In the absence of a reply we will
