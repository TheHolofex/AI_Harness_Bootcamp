## Closure

The road through the Marrow Cut will be closed to all traffic between chainage
0+900 and chainage 1+600 for the second phase of the drainage scheme.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| First day of closure | Monday 20 July 2026 |
| Last day of closure | Friday 14 August 2026 |
| Closure hours | 2000Z to 0600Z daily, including weekends |
| Daytime | Single-lane working under portable signals, 0600Z to 2000Z |
| Full-day closures | Saturday 1 August and Saturday 8 August, 0600Z to 1800Z |

Outside the hours above the road is open, under single-lane working. The signals
are automatic and will be attended during the day and left running overnight only
when the road is open.

The night closure has been set to fall inside the hours when the corridor is not
worked. Checkpoint 2 passes no traffic between 2200Z and 0500Z under MEM-2026-041
and Route AMBER is worked as a daylight corridor, so the contractor has been
instructed to take the disruptive work at night and hand the road back for the
working day. The two full-day Saturday closures are the exception and cannot be
avoided: the culvert connections at chainage 1+180 and 1+420 require a continuous
pour and cannot be broken over a shift change.

## Diversion

**For light vehicles.** Divert via the Consortium estate road from the junction at
chainage 0+700, through the Consortium yard, and rejoin north of chainage 1+600 at
the field gate. The Consortium has agreed use of the estate road for the period of
the closure. The route is signed. It is single track with passing places and a
weight restriction posted at the yard entrance. Nothing above a light vehicle is to
use it.

**For all other vehicles.** There is no local diversion. Traffic that cannot use
the estate road is to be held or routed via CINDER. Movement staff are to plan on
that basis and are not to send vehicles to the Marrow Cut on the assumption that
the contractor will let them through. He will not, and he has been told not to.

**Emergency vehicles.** The contractor will open a passage for an emergency vehicle
at any hour. The site supervisor is contactable on the works number at all times
during the closure period and the barrier at the southern end will be manned
overnight for this purpose.

## Signing and traffic management

- Advance warning signs at chainage 0+400 and chainage 2+100 from 13 July.
- Diversion signs on the estate road route from 19 July.
- Portable signals at the working head, moved forward as the work progresses.
- Lighting to the working head and to both signal heads.
- The southern approach will be coned back to chainage 0+750 throughout, including
  when the road is open. Do not remove the cones.

## Points for movement staff

1. Plan for single-lane working through the whole period, not just the closure
   hours. Delay at the signals will be a few minutes in the ordinary case and
   longer when a long vehicle is being brought through.

2. The two Saturday full-day closures are hard. Nothing crosses the Cut on those
   days. If a movement has to happen on 1 or 8 August it goes by CINDER.

3. The estate road diversion is a courtesy from the Consortium and is not a public
   route. Drivers are to keep speed down through the yard, give way to Consortium
   vehicles, and not stop in the yard for any reason.

4. Damage to the estate road or to the field gate will be recharged to us. Report
   any contact to the Consortium lodge in writing on the day.
