**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

1. The visit programme for August and September is at paragraph 4. It is heavier
than usual and several visits fall in the same weeks. Detachments are to read the
whole programme, not only the entries naming their own location, because the
transport and escort load falls across everybody.

2. **Standing instructions for visits.**

2.1 Host cell or detachment provides an escort officer for the duration of the
visit. The escort is named to Headquarters not less than three working days before.

2.2 No visit is to displace an operational task. If a visit and a task collide, the
task wins and Headquarters is to be told at once so the visit can be moved.

2.3 Briefs are to be given standing, on the ground, against the thing being
discussed. Nobody is to build a presentation. Where a visitor has asked for
figures, the figures are to be current and taken from the file, not reconstructed
from memory the night before.

2.4 Visitors are not to be taken into a works area without the contractor's site
engineer present.

2.5 Catering for visits is a working lunch from the central kitchen unless
Headquarters says otherwise. Nobody is to arrange anything more elaborate.

3. **Transport.** All visits are met at Station Halberd unless the entry says
otherwise. Movement Desk will task vehicles from the pool. Where a visit crosses
the Selwyn, movement staff are to check the crossing arrangements at the time of
planning against the operator's current published card, noting that the operator's
maintenance day is the first Wednesday of the month.

5. **Points of friction in the programme.**

5.1 Colonel Braithwaite's request to speak to detachments without their commanders
present is normal for a manning visit and detachment commanders are to facilitate
it without comment.

5.2 Brigadier Selkirk's visit on 2 September is timed to see the end of the working
period at Checkpoint 2. Checkpoint 2 passes no traffic between 2200Z and 0500Z
under MEM-2026-041 and the checkpoint is manned to standard through the working
period only. The detachment is to run the close-down normally and not to alter it
for the visit.

5.3 The audit team on 15 to 17 September will want the Consortium hire agreement,
the ferry carriage agreement, the Ardwick framework, and current liability
certificates. Finance Cell to have these assembled and indexed by 10 September.

5.4 Two visits fall on the same day at Node D in the week of 15 September. The
detachment is to host both and Headquarters will not move either.

6. Detachments are to acknowledge receipt and to confirm escort officers for the
entries affecting them by 28 July 2026.
