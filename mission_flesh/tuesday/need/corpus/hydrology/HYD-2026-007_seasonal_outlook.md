# HYD-2026-007 - Selwyn basin, seasonal outlook

**Issued:** 2026-07-02
**Issuing authority:** Basin Hydrology Cell
**Confidence:** moderate for the season, low for any given day
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## Summary

The Selwyn is a rain-fed river with a pronounced autumn rise. This outlook covers
the period to the end of the year and should be read alongside the gauge series,
which is where the numbers are.

## Season so far

Spring melt was unremarkable and the summer recession has run close to the
ten-year median. Gauge readings through June and July have sat in the range the
basin normally sees at this point in the year. Nothing in the record to date is
unusual.

## Autumn

The autumn rise is the dominant feature of the year in this basin and it is
reliable in timing if not in magnitude. It begins in the first half of September
and runs through October, with the highest readings of the year normally falling
in the second half of September or the first week of October.

The rise is driven by frontal rain over the upper catchment. Response time from
rain to gauge at the Ferry Point reach is short, in the order of eighteen to
thirty hours, so there is little useful warning within the season.

**We are not going to put a figure on the September peak in this document.** The
day-by-day forecast values are published in the gauge series and are revised
weekly. Any planner who needs a number for a specific date should take it from
the series and should take it recently, because it moves.

## What this means for river users

Anyone whose operation has a river state limit should expect that limit to bite
somewhere in the autumn rise. That is a normal year, not a bad one. Operations
that work through August without difficulty routinely find September harder, and
planning that is built on August experience tends to be optimistic.

We are asked every year whether the rise can be predicted precisely enough to
plan a specific window inside it. It cannot. It can be planned around by
accepting that some days in the window will be lost and asking how many.

## Confidence

Moderate confidence in the shape and timing of the rise. Low confidence in the
magnitude on any individual day. The series carries the day-by-day figures with
the basis marked, and the basis matters: readings to the end of August are
observed, everything after that is forecast.
