# FIN-2026-012 - Ferry charter, standing cost and variation options

**Issued:** 2026-08-11
**Issuing authority:** Finance cell, Task Group MERIDIAN
**Reference:** CON-2026-002
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## Purpose

The route decision has a cost dimension that is not obvious from the route
comparison, and the finance cell was asked to set it out.

## The standing cost

The charter at CON-2026-002 carries a **minimum charge of four lift days per
calendar month**, at 9,600 exercise credits per lift day. That is 38,400 credits
a month whether the group calls a single lift or none.

The group has called the crossing eleven times since January. It has paid for
thirty-two lift days over the same period. The gap is the minimum charge.

This is a sunk cost in the sense that it is being paid either way. It is not a
sunk cost in the sense that it makes CINDER free; the marginal cost of a CINDER
serial is the fuel, the staging fee and the escort hours, and the lift-day charge
falls on days called above the minimum.

## What the comparison does and does not include

The route cost comparison prices eleven CINDER serials against six AMBER serials.
The difference in serial count is not a finance assumption; it comes from the
Movement Desk's view of how much can be moved per serial on each corridor. The
finance cell has not tested it and flags that the whole comparison turns on it.

If the CINDER serial count is wrong, the comparison is wrong by roughly 18,500
credits per serial in either direction.

## Suspended days

Under the charter, days on which the operator suspends the crossing count against
the monthly minimum. In cost terms a suspended day inside the minimum is free and
a suspended day above the minimum is charged at the full lift-day rate.

The finance cell does not have a view on how many suspended days to expect in the
rotation window and has not been given one. **If the operational planning
produces that number, the cost comparison should be rebuilt with it, because it
moves the CINDER total materially.**

## Variation options

1. **Reduce the minimum charge.** Approached in April. The operator declined; the
   minimum is what makes the crossing viable for them out of season.
2. **Suspend the charter for the winter and re-let.** Saves the minimum but the
   operator has indicated the re-let rate would be higher.
3. **Leave as is.** Recommended. The charter is cheap insurance while AMBER
   carries a structural question mark over it.
