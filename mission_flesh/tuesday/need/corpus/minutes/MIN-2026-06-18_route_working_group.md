## 2. Matters arising

**2.1** The action on the Marrow Cut clearance figure is closed. The survey has
reported and the 4.1 metre figure stands. The chair asked that the older figure
stop appearing in planning products and was told it probably would not, because
it is embedded in a spreadsheet nobody owns.

**2.2** The action on the Selwyn Bridge rating is not closed. The Basin Structures Cell
confirmed the re-rating document has issued and that it changes the figure
planners have been using. The chair asked how many current planning products
still carry the old figure. The cell did not know. The Movement Desk thought
"most of them".

## 3. Route AMBER

The Movement Desk reported the corridor as running well. Four serials in the
period, no failures.

The desk raised the overnight closure at Checkpoint 2 as the single biggest
constraint on how the corridor can be used. Under MEM-2026-041 the checkpoint
passes no traffic between 2200Z and 0500Z, which makes AMBER a daylight corridor
and pushes every serial into the same narrow band of the morning.

The chair asked whether the manning position had improved. The Movement Desk said
a second controller detachment had arrived and that the position was better than
it had been in March. **Action: the Movement Desk to establish whether the
closure is still required and, if not, to seek its rescission.**

## 4. Route CINDER

The Liaison Desk reported on the ferry charter. The crossing is available, the
operator is co-operative, and the charter runs to January.

The consortium representative said the consortium's own traffic uses the crossing
routinely and finds it reliable. Asked about river state, the representative said
the operator stands the crossing down sometimes but could not say on what basis
or how often.

The Liaison Desk was asked to get the operator's limits in writing rather than by
report. **Action: Liaison Desk to write to the operator and file the reply.**

## 5. Structural works

The Basin Structures Cell reported that the stringer and deck plate works are
specified under the framework with **Ardwick Structural** and that a task order
can be raised at any time. The binding question is not funding but whether the
contractor can field the crew.

**Action: Basin Structures Cell to obtain the contractor's availability and
report to this group.**

The chair observed that if the works could be done before the autumn rotation the
rating question would resolve itself, and asked for the answer at the next
meeting.
