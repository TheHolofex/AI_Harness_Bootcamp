3.2 **Reasons for slippage.** Petrakis, V. gave the following.

(a) EWB/26/02, Ferry Point drainage. Two of three tenders were non-compliant. Award is held pending a re-tender of the outfall element. He expects to award in June.

(b) EWB/26/03, Node B perimeter fencing. The scheme was drawn against a survey that proved to be out of date; the northern run is longer than scheduled and the specification is being redrawn. He does not expect the cost to change materially.

(c) EWB/26/08, Checkpoint 2 lighting. Not tendered because the Estate Office has had one clerk absent since April. This is a resourcing slip and nothing more.

3.3 The Chair said that three schemes slipping out of seven was not a good return at the quarter point and asked that the position be recovered before the next meeting. Petrakis, V. accepted that.

3.4 Quinlan, T. asked to record that the bund scheme at Bastion Reach had been completed to his satisfaction and that the associated safety item was closed.

## 4. Node D hardstanding, tender over estimate

4.1 Bergstrom, A. reported that the lowest compliant tender for EWB/26/01 was 57,400 credits against an estimate of 42,000, an excess of 15,400 credits.

4.2 The tender return exceeds the estimate chiefly on the sub-base. The tenderers have all priced for a deeper excavation than the estimate assumed, on the basis that the eastern bays have failed at formation rather than in the surface course. Okonjo, B. said the tenderers were probably right, that a surface-only repair would fail again within a season, and that he would not recommend re-scoping the work downward.

4.3 Sandoval, E. said the excess could not be met from within the scheme and that the Board must either abandon the scheme, hold it to next year, or vire funds from elsewhere in the programme.

4.4 Iyer, S. said that abandoning or deferring the scheme would be poor from a movement point of view. Bays 24 to 31 are already restricted to light vehicles, Route CINDER is carrying load that was previously scheduled on Route AMBER, and Node D is the pinch point on that corridor. He asked the Board to find the money.

## 5. Virement request

5.1 The Estate Manager tabled a virement request in the following terms.

**Virement request EWB/V/26/01**

| From | Amount | To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EWB/26/06 Bastion Reach ablutions refurbishment | 16,000 credits | EWB/26/01 Node D hardstanding patch repairs |

5.2 The proposal is that EWB/26/06 be withdrawn from this year's programme in its entirety, releasing 33,900 credits, of which 16,000 is applied to EWB/26/01 and the balance of 17,900 returned to uncommitted programmable allocation.

5.3 Ashby, P. objected on welfare grounds. The ablutions at Bastion Reach are the poorest accommodation-related facility on the estate and the detachment has raised the matter twice. He accepted that the facility is serviceable and that no safety case attaches to it, but asked that the withdrawal be recorded as a deferral to next year with a commitment rather than a simple deletion.

5.4 The Chair accepted that. The minute is to record that EWB/26/06 is deferred to the next works year and is to be carried at the head of the B list at the first meeting of that year.

5.5 The virement was approved. Sandoval, E. countersigned.

## 6. Uncommitted allocation

6.1 The Board considered what to do with the 49,450 credits now uncommitted.

6.2 **Partial roof scheme.** Petrakis, V. tabled the estimate at EWB/A/26/01: 24,600 credits to renew the covering over the affected bay only, with edge protection and scaffold included as directed. Ashby, P. supported. Approved and programmed for tender in Q3.

6.3 **Node D southern perimeter fence.** Referred by the Route Working Group at RWG/26/022. Estimate 6,300 credits to replace two collapsed post sections and remove the temporary barrier. Approved.

6.4 **Node B holding area lighting.** Referred by the Route Working Group at RWG/26/018. Estimate 12,400 credits. Iyer, S. explained that the holding area is now used by serials assembling before first light, Route AMBER being worked as a daylight corridor with Checkpoint 2 passing no traffic between 2200Z and 0500Z under MEM-2026-041, and that the existing lighting was specified for a different pattern of use. Approved.

6.5 **Node D western exit and one-way circuit.** Referred by the Route Working Group at RWG/26/021. Estimate 26,900 credits. The Board could not fund this alongside the schemes at 6.2 to 6.4 and declined it for this year. Iyer, S. asked that it be carried to next year's proposals. Agreed.

6.6 Remaining uncommitted programmable allocation after the above: 6,150 credits. The Chair directed that no further scheme be approved from it without reference to him.
