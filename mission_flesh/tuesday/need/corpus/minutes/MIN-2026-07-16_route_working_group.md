## 3. September rotation

The chair introduced the September rotation as the next decision of substance.
The lift is larger than anything run this year. A route has to be named by
22 August.

The Movement Desk's position is that AMBER is the corridor the group knows and
should be the primary. The desk noted that the corridor is worked as a daylight
route because of the checkpoint closure, and that this constrains how many
serials can be run in a day but has not stopped anything so far.

The Liaison Desk's position is that CINDER should be considered seriously because
the charter is paid for whether it is used or not, and because AMBER concentrates
everything on a single structure.

The finance cell noted that the charter carries a minimum monthly charge that the
group is paying regardless, and asked that this be reflected in any comparison.
