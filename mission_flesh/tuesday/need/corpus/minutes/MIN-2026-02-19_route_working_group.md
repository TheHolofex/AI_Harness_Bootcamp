## 3. Constraints register

3.1 The Secretary reported that the first draft register carried nineteen entries, of which eleven were verified against a written authority and eight were not. The Chair directed that unverified entries be marked as such on the face of the register rather than removed.

3.2 Iyer, S. said that two of the unverified entries had been carried forward from documents that could no longer be found. Agreed that these be marked "authority not traced" and reviewed at the next meeting.

## 4. Route AMBER

4.1 The Movement Desk tabled route card AMBER/2026/1. The card was taken as read. The corridor runs north from Station Halberd through Checkpoint 2 (W-19), the Marrow Cut defile, and over the Selwyn Bridge (W-41). The bridge is rated at 40 tonnes and that figure is carried on the card.

4.2 The Chair asked for the general state of the corridor. Okonjo, B. reported that surfacing between Station Halberd and Checkpoint 2 was in fair condition with edge break-up at three points, that verge drainage on the southern approaches to the Marrow Cut was silted, and that route marking north of the defile had been damaged and was being replaced under the works programme. None of this was assessed as affecting trafficability at present.

4.3 Loughrey, K. reported that convoy running on AMBER had been routine over the reporting period, including night moves, and that no serial had been turned back. Two vehicles had been recovered for mechanical reasons, both south of Checkpoint 2.

4.4 **Marrow Cut clearance.** Iyer, S. raised a discrepancy. Route card AMBER/2026/1 records the least trafficable width at the Marrow Cut narrows as 4.4 m. A survey return held by the Movement Desk, undated but believed to be from the 2025 season, gives 4.1 m at what appears to be the same chainage. Okonjo, B. said that a third figure had at one time been quoted verbally but that he could not source it and would not offer it.

4.5 The Chair said that two figures in circulation for the same point was not acceptable and that the Group should not attempt to reason its way to the right answer from the file. He directed a physical survey.

4.6 Okonjo, B. undertook to provide a survey party. Radcliffe, J. asked that the survey be notified in advance so that route security could be adjusted; agreed. Iyer, S. asked that the survey record the chainage, the date, the surveyor and the method, and that the resulting figure be issued as the single authority, superseding both figures now held. Agreed.

## 5. Route CINDER

5.1 The Movement Desk tabled route card CINDER/2026/1. The corridor runs south-west from Station Halberd through Node D (W-07) and crosses the Selwyn by vehicle ferry at Ferry Point (W-22), operated under charter by Selwyn Ferry Services Ltd with MV Tolland Carrier.

5.2 Okonjo, B. reported that the hardstanding at Node D was showing surface break-up in the eastern bays and that patch repairs were in the works programme. The marshalling area at Ferry Point drains poorly after rain and standing water had twice been reported; a drainage scheme was also programmed.

5.3 Marchetti, D. reported that the ferry operator had run to the charter schedule over the period, with suspensions on two days when the river was up. The operator gives notice by telephone to the Movement Desk and confirms by signal. Achebe, N. confirmed that the signal path had been exercised and worked.

5.4 The Chair asked whether the notification arrangement was written down anywhere. Marchetti, D. said it was custom rather than instruction. Action to record it in the charter file and in the constraints register.

## 6. Works programme interface

6.1 Petrakis, V. reported that the Estate Works Board had met on 11 February and had prioritised the year's schemes. Several schemes touch the corridors, chiefly the Node D hardstanding repairs, the Ferry Point marshalling area drainage, and verge drainage on the Marrow Cut approaches.

6.2 The Chair asked that any works requiring a road closure be brought to the Group before the closure was arranged, not after. Petrakis, V. agreed.

6.3 Okonjo, B. reported that structural works at the Selwyn Bridge remained outstanding and awaited call-off with Ardwick Structural. No date was available and none was sought. Noted.

## 7. Signals and radio coverage

7.1 Achebe, N. reported a coverage shadow on the northern corridor between the Marrow Cut and the bridge, of some minutes' duration for a vehicle at normal convoy speed. A mast base and earthing scheme at Node D was in the works programme but would not affect the northern shadow.

7.2 The Chair asked whether the shadow had caused any operational difficulty. Loughrey, K. said not, as serials report on passing fixed points rather than continuously. Noted, no action.

## 8. External partners

8.1 Adeyemi, F. joined for this item. He reported that consortium vehicle movements on both corridors continued at about the level of the previous quarter, that consortium drivers had been briefed on MERIDIAN route cards, and that he would welcome sight of the constraints register.

8.2 The Chair agreed to share the register in redacted form, deleting entries relating to route security. Radcliffe, J. to review before issue.

8.3 Adeyemi, F. asked about the hardstanding hire arrangement at Node D. The Chair said that this was a finance matter and would be taken at the April meeting.

## 9. Finance

9.1 Bergstrom, A. reported that the escort and fuel planning rates last revised in January remained in force and that no in-year change was proposed.

9.2 No expenditure was referred under paragraph 2.2(e) of the terms of reference.
