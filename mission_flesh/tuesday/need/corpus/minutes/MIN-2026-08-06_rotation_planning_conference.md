# MIN-2026-08-06 - September rotation planning conference

**Meeting:** September rotation planning conference
**Date:** 2026-08-06
**Location:** Station Halberd
**Chair:** Chief of Staff, Task Group MERIDIAN
**Present:** all desks; Basin Structures Cell; Basin Hydrology Cell; finance
cell; Cordell Basin Relief Consortium (Adeyemi, F.); Selwyn Ferry Services
(Dunphy, M.) for item 4 only
**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

## 1. Purpose

To settle the planning assumptions for the September rotation ahead of the route
decision on 22 August. This conference does not take the decision.

## 2. The lift

The Movement Desk tabled the serial composition. 148 vehicles, moving as six
serials across the window of 10 to 25 September. The composition is fixed and
will not change before the movement order.

The chair asked whether anything in the lift was unusual. The desk said the lift
was larger than August but of the same general character, with one heavy item
that the desk described as "the recovery vehicle, which is the biggest thing we
move". The mass was not stated to the conference and is in the composition
document.

## 3. Route AMBER

The Movement Desk presented AMBER as the preferred primary on the strength of the
August running.

The Basin Structures Cell intervened to say that the corridor limits in the
Movement Desk's planning products are out of date, that this has been raised
twice, and that the September lift is the first one where it will make a
practical difference. The chair asked whether that changed the recommendation.
The desk said it did not think so but would check.

**Action: check the corridor limits against the current rating document before
the decision.** Owner recorded as "the desk". The conference did not establish
which desk, and the action was not allocated to a named person.

## 4. Route CINDER

The operator attended for this item.

The operator confirmed the crossing is available through the rotation window
subject to river state, that the vessel works daylight only, and that the
operator has given the group its limits in writing. The operator said, and asked
that it be minuted, that **the group should work from the letter on file and not
from the operator's brochure, which is out of date in more than one respect**.

The consortium representative said the consortium's traffic crosses regularly and
that in the representative's experience the crossing is reliable. Asked directly
whether that experience covered September, the representative said it did not;
the consortium's own movements are concentrated in the summer.

The finance cell tabled the route cost comparison.

## 5. The season

The Basin Hydrology Cell was asked whether the rotation window was a good one.

The cell said the window sits inside the autumn rise, that the rise is the
dominant feature of the basin year, and that the cell would not give a figure for
any individual day in a meeting. The cell said the day-by-day forecast is
published in the gauge series, is revised weekly, and should be read for the
specific dates in the window rather than summarised.

The chair asked for "a sense of it". The cell declined to give one and repeated
that the numbers are in the series.

## 6. Decision timetable

Route decision 22 August. Movement order to follow within 48 hours.
