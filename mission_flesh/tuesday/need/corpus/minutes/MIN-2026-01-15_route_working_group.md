## 1. Opening and constitution

1.1 The Chair opened the meeting at 0900Z and welcomed members. He recorded that the Group was constituted by Commander TG MERIDIAN's direction of 8 January 2026 and that its first task was to agree its own terms of reference before taking any route business of substance.

1.2 The Chair noted apologies as listed above. The meeting was quorate.

## 2. Terms of reference

2.1 The Secretary tabled draft terms of reference. After discussion the following were agreed without amendment other than at 2.2(e), which was added at the request of Finance.

2.2 The Route Working Group is to:

(a) maintain a single agreed description of each supply corridor operated by Task Group MERIDIAN, currently Route AMBER and Route CINDER, including route cards, chainages, holding areas and turn-round points;

(b) hold the register of standing constraints on each corridor, and ensure that a constraint recorded in the register is reflected in movement orders, route cards and the daily tasking sheet;

(c) coordinate the road and hardstanding works programme with movement requirements, so that works closures are notified before they are imposed and not after;

(d) act as the single point of contact with the Cordell Basin Relief Consortium, Selwyn Ferry Services Ltd and Ardwick Structural on matters affecting corridor use;

(e) refer any matter with a financial consequence above 5,000 credits to Finance before commitment, and record the referral in the minutes;

(f) report by exception to Commander TG MERIDIAN, and in any event render a short written return after each meeting.

2.3 It was agreed that the Group has no authority to vary a movement order, and no authority to commit expenditure. Its output is advice and a record.

## 3. Membership

3.1 Core membership is as at Attendance above, less invited observers.

3.2 Adeyemi, F. (Consortium) is invited as an observer for items touching shared infrastructure. Iyer, S. asked whether the observer should be present for the whole meeting or only for the relevant items. It was agreed that the Chair would decide meeting by meeting and that the minute would record which items the observer attended.

3.3 Members may send a substitute. A substitute is to be briefed and is to be named in the attendance record. Loughrey, K. asked that substitutes be sent only where the substitute can speak for the desk. Agreed.

## 4. Frequency, quorum and papers

4.1 The Group will meet monthly, on the third Thursday, at 0900Z, in Conference Room 2. Where the third Thursday is a stand-down day the Secretary will move the meeting to the following Thursday and notify members.

4.2 Quorum is the Chair or a nominated deputy, plus five members, of whom one must be the Movement Desk.

4.3 Papers are to reach the Secretariat by 1600Z on the Monday before the meeting. Papers arriving later will be held to the following month unless the Chair rules otherwise. Sandoval, E. observed that late papers had been a persistent problem on the previous standing committee. The Chair asked that the rule be applied.

## 5. Relationship with other standing bodies

5.1 The Estate Works Board holds the works budget and priorities. The Group advises the Board on movement consequences but does not set works priority.

5.2 The Safety Committee holds accident and near-miss business. Route-related near misses are to be reported to the Safety Committee in the normal way and are not to be re-run in this Group.

5.3 Marchetti, D. noted that the consortium runs its own coordination meeting and that agendas had in the past overlapped. He undertook to bring the consortium meeting dates to the Secretariat so that the two could be sequenced.

## 8. Route AMBER - initial description

8.1 The Movement Desk gave a short factual description for the record. Route AMBER runs north from Station Halberd through Checkpoint 2 (W-19), the Marrow Cut defile, and crosses the river Selwyn at the Selwyn Bridge (W-41). The bridge carries a load rating of 40 tonnes.

8.2 No decision was sought and none was taken. The Chair asked that a full route card be tabled in February.

## 9. Route CINDER - initial description

9.1 Route CINDER runs south-west from Station Halberd through Node D (W-07) and crosses the Selwyn by vehicle ferry at Ferry Point (W-22). The crossing is operated under charter by Selwyn Ferry Services Ltd using MV Tolland Carrier.

9.2 As at Item 8, a full route card is to be tabled in February.

## 10. Constraints register

10.1 Iyer, S. said that constraints were at present held in three places: the movement order, the detachment standing orders and the route cards, and that the three did not always agree. He proposed a single register held by the Secretariat and reissued after each meeting.

10.2 Agreed. The register is to carry, for each entry, the constraint, the authority for it, the date it took effect and the desk responsible.

11.2 Achebe, N. had no business.
