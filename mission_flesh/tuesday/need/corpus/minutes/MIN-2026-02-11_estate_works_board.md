## 1. Opening

1.1 The Chair opened at 1330Z. The purpose of the meeting was to set the works programme for the year against the allocation notified in FIN-2026-004.

## 2. Allocation

2.1 Sandoval, E. confirmed the allocation for the works year at 210,000 credits, of which 10,000 is held as contingency and is not available for programming at the start of the year. Programmable allocation is therefore 200,000 credits.

2.2 The Chair asked whether the contingency could be released early against a strong case. Sandoval, E. said it could, on the Chair's authority and with Finance countersignature, but advised against releasing it before the first quarter had run.

## 4. Discussion of priority

4.1 **EWB/26/01, Node D hardstanding.** Okonjo, B. said the eastern bays were breaking up and that the rate of deterioration had increased over the winter. Iyer, S. supported Priority A, saying Node D was already the pinch point on Route CINDER and that losing bays would push the queue onto the approach road. Agreed A.

4.2 **EWB/26/02, Ferry Point drainage.** Standing water in the marshalling area has been reported twice. Quinlan, T. said this was a slip and skid risk on a slope and that he would support A on safety grounds. Agreed A.

4.3 **EWB/26/03, Node B fencing.** Petrakis, V. said the northern run was largely collapsed and had been patched three times. Agreed A.

4.4 **EWB/26/04, Bastion Reach bund.** Quinlan, T. said the bund at the fuel point had a cracked upstand and would not retain a full spill. He pressed for early execution and said that if it were not programmed he would raise it as a safety item in its own right. Agreed A, and the Board asked that it be done first.

4.5 **EWB/26/05, accommodation roof.** The largest single scheme. Petrakis, V. said the covering was at the end of its life and that there had been three ingress reports in the winter, all in the same bay. Ashby, P. had submitted a written note in support on welfare grounds. Sandoval, E. observed that at 61,000 credits this scheme alone consumes nearly a third of the programmable allocation and that the Board should decide whether it wants the roof or most of the rest of the programme. After discussion the Board agreed to hold it at B and to seek a partial scheme covering the affected bay only, with an estimate to be produced.

4.6 **EWB/26/06, Bastion Reach ablutions.** Held at B. Petrakis, V. said the facility was serviceable but tired.

4.7 **EWB/26/07, Marrow Cut verge drainage.** Iyer, S. said the southern approaches were silted and that water was standing on the carriageway edge. Agreed B, to be executed in the first half of the year while the works window is favourable.

4.8 **EWB/26/08, Checkpoint 2 lighting.** Agreed B without discussion.

4.9 **EWB/26/09 to EWB/26/12.** Held at C. The Chair said C schemes would be executed only if funds released later in the year.

4.10 Iyer, S. withdrew after Item 5.

## 6. Contracts

6.1 Bergstrom, A. said that four of the programmed schemes would go to competitive tender and that the remainder were below the threshold and could be placed against the standing measured term arrangement.

6.2 He reminded the Board that any scheme touching the Selwyn Bridge structure falls outside the estate works arrangement and sits with Ardwick Structural under separate contract. Structural works there remain outstanding and await call-off. EWB/26/11 covers approach signage and barriers only and does not touch the structure.

## 7. Safety items

7.1 Quinlan, T. reported that the Safety Committee had raised no estate item since its last meeting other than the bund at EWB/26/04.

7.2 He asked that scaffold and edge protection requirements be written into the roof scheme when it is estimated. Noted.

## 8. Any other business

8.1 Tan, W. asked whether the waste compound extension at EWB/26/09 could be brought forward given that the compound is regularly over-full. The Chair said it would be reconsidered if funds released.

8.2 Petrakis, V. reported that the vehicle wash-down point at Station Halberd, referred by the Route Working Group, had been inspected and would be repaired from the small works budget rather than programmed. Noted.

8.3 Braithwaite, C. had no business.
