## Scope

This is an extract from the estate near miss register covering entries raised between 1 January and 31 July 2026 and closed or open at the date of issue. Entries relating to personnel matters, to partner organisations' internal reporting, and to two incidents still under investigation by others are withheld from this extract; the reference numbers are therefore not continuous. Severity is assessed on the standing three-point scale (Low, Moderate, High) on worst credible outcome, not on outcome as it occurred.

Reporting remains voluntary and no entry in this register attaches blame to any individual. Detachments are reminded that a near miss is reportable whether or not anything was damaged.

## Notes

1. Twenty-one entries are extracted here from a register of twenty-nine raised in the period. Of the twenty-one, three are assessed High, twelve Moderate and six Low.
2. Ferry Point (W-22) accounts for three entries, all of them on or beside the slips and all of them either a slip, a roll or a line. The pattern is consistent enough that a standing note has been issued (SAF-2026-016).
3. Two entries remain open at the date of issue. NM-2026-028 will close when the recommendations at SAF-2026-011 are dispositioned. NM-2026-029 will close on receipt of the column survey.
4. Reporting volume is up on the same period in 2025. The safety cell reads this as better reporting rather than worse practice and does not intend to draw any other conclusion from it.
5. Detachments are asked to review the entries at their own location with their people at the next available opportunity and to return a one-line confirmation to the safety cell.
