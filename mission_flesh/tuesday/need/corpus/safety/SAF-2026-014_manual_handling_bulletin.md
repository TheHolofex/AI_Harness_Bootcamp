# Safety Bulletin: Manual Handling in Stores

Reference: SAF-2026-014
Issued: 6 July 2026
Issued by: Safety Cell, Task Group MERIDIAN, Station Halberd
To: All stores staff, Station Halberd, Node B (W-04) and Node D (W-07)
Action: display on the stores noticeboard and brief at the next shift start
Display until: 6 October 2026

**Classification:** TRAINING SYNTHETIC - EXERCISE NORTHWIND SHELF. All units, organisations, people, places and events in this document are invented.

---

Three of the last eight near miss reports from stores have been lifting. None of them hurt anybody. The next one might.

**Plan the lift before you touch the load.** Where is it going, is the route clear, is the door open, and is there anything on the floor between here and there.

**Use the trolleys.** There are four at Station Halberd and two at each node. If they are all in use, wait. If they are all broken, report it, and until it is fixed treat every load as a two-person lift.

**Two people for anything over 23 kg, anything awkward, and anything above shoulder height regardless of weight.** Weight is not the only thing that hurts people. Shape and reach do most of the damage.

**Nothing heavy above shoulder height on the racking.** Heavy goes on the bottom two lifts. Light and infrequent goes high. If you find heavy stock stored high, move it down when you next handle it and tell the storeman.

**Do not climb the racking.** The steps are by the roller door. Fetch them.

**Break the load down.** Two trips is not a failure.

| Do | Do not |
| --- | --- |
| Get the load close to your body before you lift | Twist while carrying |
| Bend the knees, keep the back its natural shape | Bend and twist together |
| Get a firm grip, test the weight first | Assume the box weighs what the last one weighed |
| Move your feet to turn | Reach across a bench for a load |
| Ask for a hand | Prove something |

Report anything that hurt, twinged or nearly went wrong, whether or not you needed to see anybody about it. A report costs you five minutes. A back costs you months.

Queries to the safety cell or to your storeman.
