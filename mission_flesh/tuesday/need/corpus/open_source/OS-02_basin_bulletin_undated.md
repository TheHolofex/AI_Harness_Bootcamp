## Crossings roundup

Both of the basin's vehicle crossings are under pressure this season and users
should plan accordingly.

**The Selwyn Bridge is restricted to light traffic.** Heavy vehicles are
advised to use the southern crossing. The restriction follows an engineering
assessment, the details of which have not been published.

**The Ferry Point crossing is operating normally** and the operator has capacity
for additional traffic. The vessel is understood to be certified for heavy
vehicles up to thirty tonnes, which would make it the only crossing in the basin
able to take the heaviest traffic while the bridge restriction is in place.

Users have reported delays at Checkpoint 2 on the northern route, where the
overnight closure remains in force.

## Comment

It is not clear why the authorities continue to route heavy traffic north when
the southern crossing is available and the northern structure is restricted. The
Bulletin has asked and has not had an answer.
