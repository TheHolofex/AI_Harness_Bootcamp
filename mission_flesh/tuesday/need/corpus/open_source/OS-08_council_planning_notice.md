Notice is hereby given that the council has received the application described below and that the application is available for public inspection.

**Applicant:** Selwyn Ferry Services Ltd, Slipway Office, Ferry Point.

**Agent:** Halstow and Pyne, chartered surveyors, 14 Market Row, Corbery.

**Site:** Land adjoining the C412 Ferry Point approach road, Ferry Point, extending to approximately 0.7 hectares of rough grazing and existing informal parking between the approach road and the field boundary.

**Proposal:** Formation of a hardstanding vehicle waiting area with bound surface and line marking; relocation of the existing ticket kiosk; erection of six lighting columns of 6 metres nominal height; surface water drainage works including a Class 1 full retention oil interceptor and connection to the existing outfall; boundary fencing to the northern and eastern edges; associated signage, kerbing and landscaping.

**Application type:** Full planning permission.

**Date received:** 6 May 2026. **Date registered as valid:** 14 May 2026.

## Inspection

The application and the deposited documents may be inspected free of charge at the Corbery Offices, Development Control counter, on Monday to Thursday between 0900 and 1600 and on Friday between 0900 and 1230, excluding public holidays. Copies of individual drawings may be obtained on payment of the council's published copying charge.

## Representations

Any person may make representations in writing. Representations must:

1. quote the application reference CB/2026/0147;
2. state the name and postal address of the person making them;
3. be received by the council not later than **18 June 2026**.

Representations received after that date will be taken into account only if the application has not by then been determined. Anonymous representations will not be considered. Representations are not treated as confidential and will be published on the council's website with postal addresses and signatures redacted. Representations expressing support are considered on the same footing as objections.

Matters that the council may take into account include the effect on highway safety, the effect on residential amenity including light spill, drainage and flood risk, landscape and visual effect, and ecology. Matters that the council may not take into account include the identity or commercial interests of the applicant, the effect on the value of neighbouring property, and loss of a private view.

## Determination

The application will be determined by the Development Control Committee at its meeting on 9 July 2026, or under the Head of Development Control's delegated powers if no more than two representations contrary to the officer recommendation have been received.
