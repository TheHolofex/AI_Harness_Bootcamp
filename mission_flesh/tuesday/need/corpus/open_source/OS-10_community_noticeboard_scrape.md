**LOST DOG**

Bramble, brindle lurcher bitch, thin, wearing a green collar with no tag on it because the tag came off in the hedge in the spring and I never replaced it. Last seen going up the track behind the mill at speed after something. She is friendly but she will not come to you and she will not be caught, so please do not chase her. Ring the number and I will come. She has been out three nights now.

**SELWYN VALLEY SINGERS**

Rehearsals Thursdays 1930 in the village hall. New members always welcome and no audition of any kind. We are particularly short of men, as we are every year, and particularly short of anyone who can read music, though most of us cannot either. Subscription 30 credits a term, waived if that is a difficulty. Autumn programme is the Faure and some carols.

---

**FARMERS MARKET**

First Saturday of the month, 0900 to 1300, on the green. Stalls wanted. We have vegetables, eggs, bread and the cheese man. We do not have honey, preserves, plants or fish and we would like all four. Pitch is 12 credits, tables provided, book with the parish clerk. If it rains we go in the hall.

**CONVOY NOISE ON THE NORTH ROAD**

I want to know who I complain to and I want a name, not a switchboard.

For months now there have been heavy vehicles gathering on the lay-by at the top of the north road from about half past three in the morning. Engines left running the whole time. Reversing alarms. Men shouting to each other, not unpleasantly, just at the volume men shout at that hour when they think there is nobody about. Then at around five the lot of them pull away together and it is quiet again, by which point the entire row is awake and there is no point going back to bed.

This is on the route the signs call the AMBER corridor. I am told the gate up the road does not open until five, so they have to be sat there ready. I accept that. What I do not accept is that the place they sit and wait has to be the one lay-by within earshot of houses, when there is hardstanding a mile further on with nothing near it but sheep.

I have written once and had a polite letter back. I would like to know who else has complained, because I suspect one voice gets a polite letter and six voices get something done.

**PIANO, FREE TO ANYONE WHO WILL MOVE IT**

Upright, overstrung, needs tuning and probably needs more than tuning. It is in the front room and the front room has a door in it, which is the whole difficulty. Two strong people and a stair trolley. It is yours.

**HEDGE AND VERGE CUTTING**

Flail work, tractor hedge cutting, ditching. Local, insured, twenty years at it. I do not do gardens and I do not do stumps. Leave a message and I will get back to you when I am off the machine, which may be a while at this time of year.

---

**DEFIBRILLATOR TRAINING**

A second session has been arranged at the village hall following the demand for the first. Free, about an hour, no booking, just turn up. The machine on the hall wall is available to anyone at any time and you do not need training to use it. Come anyway. The point of the evening is that you will be less frightened of it.

---

**BENCH TAKEN FROM THE GREEN**

The oak bench with the brass plate has gone from the green. It was there on the Sunday and gone on the Monday. It was not rotten, it was not being replaced, and nobody on the parish council authorised its removal. It was given in memory of somebody and the family would like it back. No questions will be asked if it reappears.

---

**BABYSITTING**

Sixteen, sensible, first aid certificate, references from three families in the village. Evenings and weekends. I do not drive so I would need collecting from Nether Selwyn or dropping back.

---

**WANTED: RUNNERS FOR THE MOBILE LIBRARY**

If you have a neighbour who cannot get to the van, we are looking for people willing to take books to and from. It is twenty minutes a fortnight. We currently have four volunteers covering eleven households and one of the four is moving away in the autumn.
