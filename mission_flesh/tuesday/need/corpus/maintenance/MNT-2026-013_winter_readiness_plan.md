## 1. Intent

To have the estate, the plant and the standby rosters ready before the first
frosts, so that neither corridor is lost to a condition the section could have
prepared for. Preparation is done through the autumn, on the planned maintenance
schedule at MNT-2026-002, and is complete before the season starts rather than
during it.

## 2. Gritting

a. **Treated network.** In priority order:

| Priority | Stretch or area | Treated by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Station Halberd main access road and gate approaches | Section gritter |
| 1 | Route AMBER carriageway, Station Halberd to Checkpoint 2 | Section gritter |
| 1 | Marrow Cut defile and its approaches | Section gritter |
| 1 | Selwyn Bridge approaches and span | Section gritter, hand spread on the span |
| 2 | Route CINDER, Junction 4 to Node D | Section gritter |
| 2 | Kellar Ridge, both directions | Section gritter, second pass on the descent |
| 2 | Ferry Point approach road and marshalling area | Detachment, hand and tractor spreader |
| 3 | Detachment site roads and vehicle parks | Detachment, hand and tractor spreader |
| 3 | Footways, walkways and building approaches | Detachment working parties |
| 4 | Overflow parking and unused hardstanding | On demand only |

b. **Trigger.** Precautionary treatment is ordered by the Duty Officer on receipt
   of a forecast of frost or ice for the following period. There is no automatic
   trigger and no self-tasking by detachments for priority 1 and 2 stretches.
   Detachments treat their own priority 3 areas on their own judgement.

c. **Timing.** Priority 1 stretches are treated to be complete before the first
   serial of the day is released. On Route AMBER this means the run finishes
   ahead of SP, and since Checkpoint 2 passes no traffic between 2200Z and 0500Z
   under MEM-2026-041 the gritter works the AMBER stretch south of the
   checkpoint in the closure period and the stretch north of it after 0500Z.

d. **Records.** Every treatment run is logged: date, time out, time in, stretches
   treated, quantity spread, operator. The log is the section's evidence and it
   is completed at the time, not afterwards.

a. All culverts, gullies, ditches and outfalls on both corridors are cleared
   through the autumn on the quarterly cycle at MNT-2026-002, with the autumn
   clear treated as the season clear and inspected on completion by the section
   rather than signed off by the contractor alone.

b. Particular attention:

| Location | Item | Why |

| Selwyn Bridge | Approach gullies and outfalls | Standing water on the approaches ices first |

| Kellar Ridge | Ditches both sides of the descent | Run off crosses the carriageway where ditches are full |

c. Leaf fall clearance on the tree lined stretch between Node D and Kellar Ridge
   is a weekly task through the autumn, run by the Node D detachment with
   section support. Cleared material is removed from site and not tipped on the
   verge.

d. Where a gully or culvert has been cleared twice in the season and blocked
   again, it is raised as a works request for investigation rather than cleared
   a third time.

Mounting trials matter. A blade that has never been fitted to the vehicle that
will carry it is not readiness, it is a blade in a store. Every plough and
mounted gritter is fitted to its nominated vehicle and driven, and the trial is
recorded by vehicle registration.

## 6. Standby arrangements

a. **Section standby.** One supervisor and two operatives on call, held on a
   weekly roster, from the start of the season to its end. Standby is paid at
   the agreed rate and the roster is published a month ahead.

b. **Gritter crews.** Two crews of two, rostered week and week about, each crew
   holding both a towed gritter driver and a mate. Crews must be current on the
   route they are treating: the driver qualification matrix at MOV-2026-079 is
   the authority and no crew treats a route it is not marked for.

c. **Detachment standby.** Each detachment nominates a snow party of not less
   than four for its own priority 3 areas, held on a local roster. Detachment
   commanders return the roster to the section before the season starts.

d. **Call out.** Through the Duty Officer only. The Duty Officer holds the
   roster, the contact numbers and the authority to call out. Detachments do not
   telephone the section directly at night.

e. **Contractor standby.** Coldwater Plant Hire holds a call out arrangement for
   plant failure under CWP/PLT/25, four hour response in working hours and next
   working day otherwise. There is no overnight contractor response and the
   section plans on its own resources between last light and first light.

f. **Escalation.** Where treatment cannot keep a priority 1 stretch open, the
   Duty Officer informs the Movement Desk before the first serial is released,
   not after. A corridor that is going to be closed is better closed early.

## 7. Contingency for Route CINDER

The crossing at Ferry Point is operated by Selwyn Ferry Services Ltd and is
suspended at the operator's discretion when the river is up. This is outside the
section's control and outside this plan. What the section can do is keep the
approach road, the marshalling area and the slip road drainage clear, so that a
serial turned round at the marshalling area can return without difficulty, and
so that the operator's own access is not the limiting factor. Both are done on
the autumn drainage clear and checked again before the season.

## 8. Completion

Detachment commanders confirm to the section, by signal, that:

1. Grit bins are filled and in position.
2. Bagged salt holdings are at target.
3. The snow party roster is nominated and published.
4. Local drainage is cleared and inspected.
5. Hand tools and spreaders are serviceable and counted.

Confirmation is by return, in that order, one signal per detachment. Nil returns
are not accepted.
