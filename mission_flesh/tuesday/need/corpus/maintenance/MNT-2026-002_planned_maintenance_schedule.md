## 1. Standing

This schedule covers planned and preventive tasks across the estate at Station
Halberd and the five detachment sites. Reactive work is not scheduled here and
is raised through the works request system. Where a contractor is named, the
task is called off against the framework hire or service agreement shown in the
contract column and the section does not carry out the work in house.

Access for any task on a running surface is coordinated with the Movement Desk
not less than five working days ahead. Tasks marked (C) require a road closure
or lane restriction.

## 4. Notes

a. Tasks marked (C) require a closure or lane restriction and must be booked
   with the Movement Desk. On Route AMBER these are worked inside the daylight
   window, since Checkpoint 2 passes no traffic between 2200Z and 0500Z under
   MEM-2026-041 and a night possession would strand the works traffic.

b. Where a task falls due in the same month as a serial-heavy period, the
   section will move the task rather than the serial. The desk has first call on
   the running surface.

c. The Selwyn Bridge stringer and deck plate works remain outstanding. They are
   held on the contract line and await call-off. Nothing in this schedule
   programmes them and no access booking has been made.

d. Contractors attending any site are met at the gate and escorted to the works
   area. Detachment commanders provide the escort from their own resources.
