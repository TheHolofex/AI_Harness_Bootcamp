# Intake · Sealift crisis playbook (staff draft)
Source: sealift working group · Received: Wed 1355Z

For crisis compression, the working group keeps **rail plus Ro/Ro sealift** as
the primary movement plan for a combat-loaded MBT. The loaded envelope remains
73.6 short tons ([[SRC-MBT-ENVELOPE]]), and the pier-to-pier planning window
remains 14–21 days ([[SRC-SEALIFT-TRANSIT]]). Airlift is reserved for smaller
components and personnel.
