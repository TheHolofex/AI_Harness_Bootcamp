# Western rail heavy-armor movement — planning note (course fixture)

Publisher: Course logistics desk (synthesized from public rail practice summaries)
Document date: 2024-06-01
Source ID: SRC-RAIL-AAR-SPEED

## Planning values
For combat-loaded main battle tank moves on commercial heavy-duty flatcars in the western United States, the **planning speed is 40 mph** once the car is loaded and inspected. Higher bulletin speeds are not the planning default for this envelope.

Plate and excess-height clearances must be coordinated before the cut leaves the origin railhead. Bridge and tunnel lists are route-specific.

## What this note does not say
This note does **not** authorize 45 mph as a loaded planning speed for the 70+ short-ton combat envelope.
