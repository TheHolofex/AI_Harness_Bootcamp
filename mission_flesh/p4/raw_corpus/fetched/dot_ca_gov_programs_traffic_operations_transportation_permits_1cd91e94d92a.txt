The Caltrans Office of Commercial Vehicle Operations is responsible for the issuance of these special transportation permits for oversize/overweight vehicles on the State Highway System. Still experiencing issues accessing a form? ) Single Trip Permit (Single Trip I and Single Trip II) &nbsp;- loads greater than 8'-6" wide, 14'-0" high, and over 80,000 pounds. Annual Permit &nbsp;- loads up to 12'-0" wide, 14'-0" high, and Kingpin to Rear Axle (KPRA) 40'-0" maximum (except as specifically allowed per CVC). Travel on red routes prohibited. Repetitive Permit - loads up to 12'-0" wide, 14'-6" high, and 90'-0" long. Sea Container Permit -&nbsp;4-Axle tractor and 3-Axle trailer of maximum Overall Length (OAL) of 65'-0", and Kingpin to Rear Axle (KPRA) 40'-0" maximum, transporting intermodal cargo containers on state highways in the vicinity of the Port of Los Angeles and the Port of Long Beach. Variance Permit - vehicles greater than 15'-0" wide, 17'-0" high, and 135'-0" long, or on special hauling equipment which exceeds the Department's standard method of weight classification. Motorsport Permit &nbsp;- vehicles going to an Automobile Competition Committee for the United States (ACCUS) sanctioned event at one of the locations currently authorized per vehicle code.
