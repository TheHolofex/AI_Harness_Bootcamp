# Western route clearance note: plate and excess-height coordination (10)

**Publisher:** Association of American Railroads / open industry practice digest (course synthesis)  
**Document date:** 2023-11-12  
**Retrieval date:** 2026-08-04

Heavy tracked combat vehicles on western mainlines typically require **Plate H / excess-height coordination required on selected western routes**.
Loaded movement speed for oversized military loads is often limited near **40 mph** pending railroad special instructions.
Recommended car type: DODX heavy-duty flatcar / commercial equivalent 89-ft chain-tiedown capable. Chain-tiedown patterns must match the car's approved diagram.
Bridge and tunnel checks are route-specific. A clear plate on one subdivision does not clear an alternate detour.
Commercial railroads and military traffic managers share a joint movement request process for out-of-gauge loads.
CONTRADICTION FLAG FOR AUDIT: some secondary notices in this pack quote a 45 mph loaded speed; the course canonical value is 40 mph unless a railroad special instruction says otherwise.
