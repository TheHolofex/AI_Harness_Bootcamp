## Afternoon — The poisoned corpus

P5 begins with a supplied trusted reference pack, staged intake, and its own local workspace. Everything needed for the afternoon is included in the P5 pack.

| Objective | Skill | Builds on | What is new | What mastery looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Decide what "trusted" means before anything arrives.** | Deciding what to trust | Monday's reality checking | Monday checked one result after it came back. This sets a standing rule for what is allowed in at all, written before anything hostile exists to test it. | You wrote the rule when nothing was at stake and it still reads sensibly now that something is. You are not making the call item by item under pressure, and it shows in how little the pressure changes you. |
| **2. Catch a false citation with a check rather than a hunch.** | Opening the cited source | Monday's claim tracing | Monday's audit caught honest mistakes. This one faces a real source made to vouch for a materially false claim. | A citation that looks right gets opened anyway. You have stopped treating plausibility as a reason to skip the check, so the false claim is caught by a step you always run rather than a suspicion you happened to have. |
| **3. Catch material that contradicts what you already hold.** | Spotting contradictions | Monday's change reading | Monday read what moved between two runs of your own work. This compares each staged item with a supplied trusted reference record and catches fields that cannot both be true. | New material meets the trusted reference pack instead of being read on its own. The clash surfaces because the system compares, not because you remembered the earlier record at the right moment. |
| **4. Prove that the named hostile effects are absent.** | Proving nothing happened | Monday's failure surfacing | Monday made a failure show up. This shows an event did not happen, which is harder — having nothing to point at looks the same as never having looked. | You compare the before/after inventory for the reference pack and staged inputs, confirm the runtime-config hash, then inspect the exported session for forbidden output, tool use, or path use. |
| **5. Tell what a permission can enforce from what only judgment can decide.** | Limits of permission | Tuesday's seeing what is loaded | Tuesday told you what a setup could reach. This draws the harder line: what a permission can stop, and what it can never settle. | You are precise about what a permission bought you and unbothered that it bought so little. Nobody hears you say the material is sound because the access was narrow. |

> **After the afternoon:** You can take in material from sources you do not control, catch what is wrong with it by a check rather than a feeling, and prove the trusted reference pack and staged inputs stayed unchanged with no forbidden output, tool use, or path use.
