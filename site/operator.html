1 · Before the mission

Direction Brief

This brief freezes the agreement before the build chat starts. Capture the outcome, what done looks like, the bounds, the evidence standard, and the stop or hand-back condition. Mark it LIVE only after those five fields are specific enough to test.

2 · After the mission

Operator Log

The log is the record a peer can audit later. Point to the artifacts and observed results, note what changed, and assign ACCEPT, REJECT, or ACCEPT WITH FIXES. A model can argue; the operator owns the verdict.

3 · Before the attack

Pass Bars

Pass Bars turn a broad success claim into statements that can survive inspection. Tie each bar to a file, command result, measurement, or visible behavior. If the proof is partial, narrow the bar before you claim it.

4 · New chat every block

Adversarial review

Open a clean chat and ask it to attack the artifact against the frozen bars. Rule each bar SURVIVES, WOUNDED, or DEAD. Fix the artifact or downgrade the verdict when the attack lands.

5 · After the attack

Measurement spine

Record the same four dimensions every half-day: ritual completion, mission result, quality after review, and time. Comparable fields matter more than false precision; use honest estimates when you did not time a segment directly.

6 · Close the half-day

Transfer 30-60-90

Choose one real workflow and decide what you will try in 30 days, stabilize in 60, and measure or retire by 90. Name the owner, next action, evidence, and gate. Transfer stays open until those fields describe work you can actually schedule.
