Block teaching page

Operator pulse (required every block)

  1. Open thread Operator — Direction & Log. Paste the brief starter. Fill all five fields: Outcome, Done looks like, Bounds, Evidence standard, Stop/hand-back. Status must be LIVE before the mission.
  2. Use a separate build thread (not the operator thread). Follow the mission steps below against your LIVE brief.
  3. Back in Direction & Log, paste the log starter. Record: what you directed, mid-run changes, verdict (ACCEPT / REJECT / ACCEPT WITH FIXES), evidence pointers, what you still own.
  4. Against this block’s MVP list: mark met / not yet with evidence pointers. No inflation.
  5. Open a new thread Adversarial — P2. Paste frozen prompt + artifacts + provisional verdict. Copy final line into log: ADVERSARIAL: stood | wounded | failed — …. Fix if wounded.
  6. Fill ritual 0–3, mission HIT/PARTIAL/MISS, quality STRONG/ADEQUATE/WEAK, time to result (minutes). Deep mark if this block has one.
  7. Thread Operator — Transfer 30-60-90. Paste every-session prompt. Session-log row required even if the seed is small.

Mission steps (do these in order)

  1. Pick engineering or mission_ops. Keep it through P8.
  2. Open instruments/p2_dyno/<track>/cases/ and score sheet.
  3. New/stock harness chat. Score each case PASS/FAIL with notes.
  4. Touch at least two of: instructions, tests, memory, skills. Not one lucky prompt tweak.
  5. Show red→green story where possible (fails stock / passes after).
  6. New thread if possible. Rescore. Compute delta on score sheet.
  7. Restart or new thread still carries craft; re-run one dyno item cold.
  8. Name walls improved and what still fails. Measurement deep mark: Dyno baseline n/5 → after n/5.
  9. Open Transfer thread; fill identity and harness/30-day seed.

Mastery floor (MVP) — evidence must survive adversarial

  1. instruments/p2_dyno D01–D05.
  2. Distinct layers.
  3. Numeric or strict P/F counts.
  4. Where possible.
  5. Cold re-run item.
  6. Not “feels smarter.”
  7. Transfer Identity + habit.

Close the block

  1. Adversarial line present; measurement row written; transfer row written; you could defend this block to a peer.
How to use with the lead The lead operates this same list on screen. You check your own box when you finish the step — not when you watch it. If adversarial wounds an MVP item, uncheck it until fixed.
