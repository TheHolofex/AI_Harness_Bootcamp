Why this block exists

A vault is a folder of linked notes (we use Obsidian). Citations you can click beat quotes the model invents. Today you organize an inbox and answer director questions with real note links.

What you will leave with

  • Scope vault access in LIVE brief
  • Inbox → navigable link graph
  • ≥2 multi-note answers with clickable citations
  • Trail audit; kill or revise at least one weak answer
  • Morning Brief note in vault; transfer knowledge seed; pulse

Before you start

  • Obsidian installed
  • Copy seeded vault from mission_flesh/p4
Every block ends with the operator pulse Direction Brief (LIVE) → Mission → Log + pass-bar draft → new Adversarial chat → Measurement spine row → Transfer pulse. See Pulse & chats for paste-ready prompts.

Run of exercise

  1. Open the chair

    Open your Codex app project. Confirm operator templates are present (DIRECTION_BRIEF.md, OPERATOR_LOG.md, etc.).

    Open chat Operator — Direction & Log. Paste the Direction Brief starter from the operator pack.

  2. Co-write the Direction Brief until LIVE

    Work the five fields with the AI: Outcome, Done looks like, Bounds, Evidence standard, Stop/hand-back.

    Do not start the mission until status is LIVE. Vague “explore the data” outcomes get challenged — name the artifact.

  3. Run the mission

    Copy mission_flesh/p4/vault_seed/ into a new Obsidian vault (or open in place).

    1. Process 00_Inbox/ into linked notes under a simple structure (People / Systems / Events / Decisions — or your IA).
    2. Ask ≥2 director questions via Codex with vault access scoped in your brief.
    3. Require clickable wiki-links to notes; open each citation; mark support / partial / not supported.
    4. Revise or kill at least one answer after trail audit.
    5. Generate Morning_Brief.md into the vault with verified links only.
    Vault root: [path]. Only read/write inside this vault.
Organize the inbox into linked notes. Then answer:
1) What are the top operational risks this week and which notes support each?
2) What decision is blocked and what evidence is missing?
Cite notes as [[Note Name]]. Do not cite notes you did not create or that lack support.

  4. Operator Log + pass-bar draft

    Back in Direction & Log, paste the log starter. Record verdict, evidence, what you still own.

    Self-check against PASS_BARS.md for P4 — met / not yet with pointers. No inflation.

  5. Adversarial review (new chat)

    Open a new chat titled Adversarial — P4. Paste the frozen prompt from the operator pack plus your artifacts and provisional verdict.

    Copy the final line into your log: ADVERSARIAL: stood | wounded | failed — …. Fix if wounded.

  6. Measurement spine

    Update MEASUREMENT_SPINE.md: ritual 0–3, mission HIT/PARTIAL/MISS, quality STRONG/ADEQUATE/WEAK, time to result (minutes).

  7. Transfer pulse

    Chat Operator — Transfer 30-60-90. Paste the every-session transfer prompt. Leave a session-log row even if the seed is small today.

Before lunch · Harness case talk (45 min) After the mission pulse for this morning, the lead runs a 30-minute real-world case: a problem they solved with harnessed AI, how the harness helped, and what the experience was — then 15 minutes of discussion. No hard wall into lunch. Students: listen for problem → harness move → evidence; leave one Transfer seed. Lead guide: lead/HARNESS_CASE_TALKS.md.

Pass bar (mastery floor)

Full checklist: operator/PASS_BARS.md · section P4. MVP is not “it ran.” Evidence must survive adversarial review.

If you get stuck

  • Narrow data scope, not the judgment standard.
  • Re-read your LIVE brief — most chaos is an unbounded outcome.
  • Ask for a lead diagram of the one concept blocking you, then continue on your chair.
