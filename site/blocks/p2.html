Why this block exists

A stock assistant forgets how you work. A modded harness carries instructions, tests, memory, and skills across sessions. The dyno is a shared five-case test (D01–D05). You score baseline, change the walls, score again. Numbers beat “feels smarter.”

What you will leave with

  • Choose track (engineering or mission_ops) and keep it all week
  • Baseline D01–D05; ≥2 wall changes; after score; red→green test story
  • Durability: new chat/restart still carries craft
  • Open Transfer file; Identity + P2 seed
  • Full pulse + dyno deep mark

Run of exercise

  1. Open the chair

    Open your Codex app project. Confirm operator templates are present (DIRECTION_BRIEF.md, OPERATOR_LOG.md, etc.).

    Open chat Operator — Direction & Log. Paste the Direction Brief starter from the operator pack.

  2. Co-write the Direction Brief until LIVE

    Work the five fields with the AI: Outcome, Done looks like, Bounds, Evidence standard, Stop/hand-back.

    Do not start the mission until status is LIVE. Vague “explore the data” outcomes get challenged — name the artifact.

  3. Run the mission

    Choose your track once: engineering or mission_ops. You will keep it through P8.

    Open instruments/p2_dyno/<track>/cases/ and the shared score sheet.

    1. Baseline: new chat, stock harness, score D01–D05 PASS/FAIL.
    2. Hot-rod: improve ≥2 walls. Each one is a real artifact in the app, not a habit you promise to keep:
      • Instructions → AGENTS.md at the project root. Loaded every session, so it is the difference between a rule you re-type and a rule the machine already has.
      • Skills → a folder with a SKILL.md. The description line is what decides whether it fires unprompted, so write it as trigger conditions rather than a title.
      • Memory → off by default. Turn it on deliberately and know it is a recall layer, not where rules that must always hold belong. Those go in AGENTS.md, which is also the only one of the two you can commit.
      • Tests → author ≥2 machine-checkable cases; show red→green if you can.
    3. After: cold new chat, rescore D01–D05. Cold matters — a wall that only works while the old chat is open is not a wall.
    4. Write which wall moved and what still fails. Start Transfer Identity + P2 seed.

    Lead will dyno live on one track; you still score your own sheet.

  4. Operator Log + pass-bar draft

    Back in Direction & Log, paste the log starter. Record verdict, evidence, what you still own.

    Self-check against PASS_BARS.md for P2 — met / not yet with pointers. No inflation.

  5. Adversarial review (new chat)

    Open a new chat titled Adversarial — P2. Paste the frozen prompt from the operator pack plus your artifacts and provisional verdict.

    Copy the final line into your log: ADVERSARIAL: stood | wounded | failed — …. Fix if wounded.

  6. Measurement spine

    Update MEASUREMENT_SPINE.md: ritual 0–3, mission HIT/PARTIAL/MISS, quality STRONG/ADEQUATE/WEAK, time to result (minutes).

  7. Transfer pulse

    Chat Operator — Transfer 30-60-90. Paste the every-session transfer prompt. Leave a session-log row even if the seed is small today.

Before lunch · Harness case talk (45 min) After the mission pulse for this morning, the lead runs a 30-minute real-world case: a problem they solved with harnessed AI, how the harness helped, and what the experience was — then 15 minutes of discussion. No hard wall into lunch. Students: listen for problem → harness move → evidence; leave one Transfer seed. Lead guide: lead/HARNESS_CASE_TALKS.md.

Pass bar (mastery floor)

Full checklist: operator/PASS_BARS.md · section P2. MVP is not “it ran.” Evidence must survive adversarial review.

If you get stuck

  • Narrow data scope, not the judgment standard.
  • Re-read your LIVE brief — most chaos is an unbounded outcome.
  • Ask for a lead diagram of the one concept blocking you, then continue on your chair.
