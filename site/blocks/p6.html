Why this block exists

Autonomy is not “the brand is safe.” It is a loop plus bounds you wrote. Some bounds the tool enforces; some you enforce by procedure. Pi shows what a bare loop looks like without rails.

What you will leave with

  • Adapt goose recipe to local feeder; one successful run
  • Two-column contract ≥3 rows each; risk mapping
  • Stop proof + restart proof; exception drill
  • Pi contrast notes from observation
  • Transfer P6 seed; pulse

Before you start

  • goose working from pre-work
  • Feeder files from mission_flesh/p6
Every block ends with the operator pulse Direction Brief (LIVE) → Mission → Log + pass-bar draft → new Adversarial thread → Measurement spine row → Transfer pulse. See Pulse & threads for paste-ready prompts.

Run of exercise

  1. Open the chair

    Open your Codex project. Confirm operator templates are present (DIRECTION_BRIEF.md, OPERATOR_LOG.md, etc.).

    Open thread Operator — Direction & Log. Paste the Direction Brief starter from the operator pack.

  2. Co-write the Direction Brief until LIVE

    Work the five fields with the AI: Outcome, Done looks like, Bounds, Evidence standard, Stop/hand-back.

    Do not start the mission until status is LIVE. Vague “explore the data” outcomes get challenged — name the artifact.

  3. Run the mission

    Feeder: mission_flesh/p6/feeder/ (dropping files simulates watch traffic).

    1. Adapt a goose recipe/session to process new feeder files into a short watch summary in out/.
    2. Write autonomy contract: columns Tool-enforced | Procedure-enforced (≥3 rows each). Map real risks.
    3. Demonstrate stop and restart.
    4. Exception drill: introduce an out-of-bounds file; show contract behavior.
    5. Open Pi on a tiny task; list rails present in goose/contract and missing in Pi.

    If scheduled runs are blocked on your OS, document why and run manual trigger — honesty over theater.

  4. Operator Log + pass-bar draft

    Back in Direction & Log, paste the log starter. Record verdict, evidence, what you still own.

    Self-check against PASS_BARS.md for P6 — met / not yet with pointers. No inflation.

  5. Adversarial review (new thread)

    Open a new thread titled Adversarial — P6. Paste the frozen prompt from the operator pack plus your artifacts and provisional verdict.

    Copy the final line into your log: ADVERSARIAL: stood | wounded | failed — …. Fix if wounded.

  6. Measurement spine

    Update MEASUREMENT_SPINE.md: ritual 0–3, mission HIT/PARTIAL/MISS, quality STRONG/ADEQUATE/WEAK, time to result (minutes).

  7. Transfer pulse

    Thread Operator — Transfer 30-60-90. Paste the every-session transfer prompt. Leave a session-log row even if the seed is small today.

Pass bar (mastery floor)

Full checklist: operator/PASS_BARS.md · section P6. MVP is not “it ran.” Evidence must survive adversarial review.

If you get stuck

  • Narrow data scope, not the judgment standard.
  • Re-read your LIVE brief — most chaos is an unbounded outcome.
  • Ask for a lead diagram of the one concept blocking you, then continue on your chair.
