Why this block exists

Not every job wants a free-roaming agent. A pipeline is a designed path: one pass, predictable stages, humans on exceptions. Today you build that path and write when you would still use an agent (P6) instead.

What you will leave with

  • n8n flow end-to-end with human approval gate
  • ≥3 exceptions adjudicated
  • Wrong-but-confident row caught by gate
  • Discrimination write-up: agent vs pipeline on your work
  • Draft 30/60/90 horizons in Transfer; pulse

Run of exercise

  1. Open the chair

    Open your Codex project. Confirm operator templates are present (DIRECTION_BRIEF.md, OPERATOR_LOG.md, etc.).

    Open thread Operator — Direction & Log. Paste the Direction Brief starter from the operator pack.

  2. Co-write the Direction Brief until LIVE

    Work the five fields with the AI: Outcome, Done looks like, Bounds, Evidence standard, Stop/hand-back.

    Do not start the mission until status is LIVE. Vague “explore the data” outcomes get challenged — name the artifact.

  3. Run the mission

    Rows: mission_flesh/p7/rows.csv (includes messy and wrong-but-confident-prone fields).

    1. Build n8n flow: read rows → AI classify/extract → validate → write sink (sheet or out/master.csv).
    2. Human-approval node before commit of uncertain/bad rows.
    3. Exercise ≥3 exceptions; adjudicate commit / fix / reject with reasons.
    4. Show one wrong-but-confident AI path caught by the gate.
    5. Write discrimination note: what stays agent (P6) vs pipeline on your real work. Draft Transfer 30/60/90 horizons.

  4. Operator Log + pass-bar draft

    Back in Direction & Log, paste the log starter. Record verdict, evidence, what you still own.

    Self-check against PASS_BARS.md for P7 — met / not yet with pointers. No inflation.

  5. Adversarial review (new thread)

    Open a new thread titled Adversarial — P7. Paste the frozen prompt from the operator pack plus your artifacts and provisional verdict.

    Copy the final line into your log: ADVERSARIAL: stood | wounded | failed — …. Fix if wounded.

  6. Measurement spine

    Update MEASUREMENT_SPINE.md: ritual 0–3, mission HIT/PARTIAL/MISS, quality STRONG/ADEQUATE/WEAK, time to result (minutes).

  7. Transfer pulse

    Thread Operator — Transfer 30-60-90. Paste the every-session transfer prompt. Leave a session-log row even if the seed is small today.

Pass bar (mastery floor)

Full checklist: operator/PASS_BARS.md · section P7. MVP is not “it ran.” Evidence must survive adversarial review.

If you get stuck

  • Narrow data scope, not the judgment standard.
  • Re-read your LIVE brief — most chaos is an unbounded outcome.
  • Ask for a lead diagram of the one concept blocking you, then continue on your chair.
