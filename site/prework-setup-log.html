Keep one record as we install together during Monday morning’s clinic. It gives you and the instructor the exact machine details, errors, fixes, versions, and proof locations needed to solve problems quickly.

How to use it

  1. Copy the template below into your notes, or print this page.
  2. After each tool, record the result and roughly how long the step took.
  3. Keep secrets out of the log. Record a key name and whether its presence check passed, never the key value.
  4. Keep the log and the proof folder open during the clinic.
