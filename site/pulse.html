Pulse & chats

The pulse is the sport. Projects are the gym. Every morning and afternoon follows the same rhythm so direction and cothinking become muscle memory.

EVERY HALF-DAY PULSE
  1. Direction Brief     (chat: Operator — Direction & Log)
  2. Mission build       (build chat)
  3. Operator Log
     + PASS_BARS draft   (Direction & Log)
  4. Adversarial review  (NEW chat every block)
  5. Measurement spine   (ritual · mission · quality · time)
  6. Transfer pulse      (chat: Operator — Transfer 30-60-90)

Why separate chats

Build chat wants to cheerlead. Operator chat holds judgment. Adversarial chat must be a clean room — new each block — so the reviewer did not just help you succeed. Transfer chat maps today’s mastery onto your real desk.
