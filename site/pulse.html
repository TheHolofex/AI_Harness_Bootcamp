Why separate chats

The build chat helps make the work. The Operator chat records your direction and decision. Start the evidence review in a fresh chat so the reviewer was not also the assistant that helped build the result. Use the Transfer chat to carry the lesson into one real work habit.

Paste-ready starters

Use the prompt on your current lesson page when you complete the reflection. These short references explain what each record must prove:

  • Before the mission → Direction Brief
  • After the exercise → Operator Log and Lesson outcomes
  • In a new chat → Independent evidence review
  • After the review → Measurement spine
  • Every AM and PM → Transfer 30-60-90
