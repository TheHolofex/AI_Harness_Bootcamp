After every lesson

Lesson reflection guide

Complete each lesson outcome beside the step that produces its evidence. At the close, review the page’s Complete / Not yet summary, record your decision, ask a fresh reviewer to challenge the evidence, save the result, and carry one useful change into your work. Your answer to the lesson’s reflection question becomes one sentence in operator/MEASUREMENT_SPINE.md; it does not need its own chat.

Start the reflection

Open the standing direction chat first.

  • Open or switch to the ChatGPT app, then select Codex.
  • Open the course project at Documents\HarnessBootcamp\AI_Harness_Bootcamp.
  • In the project sidebar, open Operator — Direction & Log. Continue the same chat you used for the lesson's Direction Brief.
  • If that chat is missing, click New chat, open its … menu, choose Rename, and enter Operator — Direction & Log. The lesson prompt can recover the written context from the course files.
  • Do not begin the reflection in the lesson's Build chat. The lesson page will tell you exactly when to change chats.

Why separate chats

The Build chat helps make the work. The Direction & Log chat records your direction and decision. Start the evidence review in a fresh chat so the reviewer was not also the assistant that helped build the result. Continue the Transfer chat to carry the lesson into one real work habit.
