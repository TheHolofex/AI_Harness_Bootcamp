# Operator Log

Standing operator file. Same project as the Direction Brief.  
When a module page uses this file, write the entry in the exact module-specific closeout chat named on that page, after the mission run. The generic direction-chat pattern is **`[MODULE] — Direction & Closeout`**: replace `[MODULE]` with the current module code and follow the page when it names a shorter closeout chat or adds a date. Saved files carry continuity; no global operator chat is required.

**How it gets written:** you and the AI produce each entry **interactively**. The AI helps reconstruct what happened and stress-tests your evidence. **You** assign the verdict. The AI may argue; it does not grade you.

Five fields. Target: under five minutes of dialogue. Append one entry per block; do not erase the week.

---

## Entry template (fields)

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **1. What I directed** | Outcome from the live Brief (one line). |
| **2. What I changed mid-run** | Redirects, stops, bound changes — or `none`. |
| **3. Verdict** | `ACCEPT` / `REJECT` / `ACCEPT WITH FIXES` |
| **4. Evidence** | What you checked (path, test, citation, rerun). Not “looked good.” |
| **5. I still own** | Judgment, next action, or risk the machine must not carry alone. |
| **6. Adversarial** | Paste final line: `ADVERSARIAL: stood / wounded / failed — …` (from review chat). |

P3 uses **6. Decision review** instead: record the most consequential source-backed disposition from the comparator and MCP evidence packet. P4 uses **6. Integrated evaluation** instead: point to the human raw-source audit, configured read-only evaluator receipt, deterministic verifier, resume receipt, terminal reason, intervention and repair counts, and residual risk. Neither live page adds a separate attack chat.

## Module-closeout starter (paste after the mission)

```text
We are writing the Operator Log entry for this block together.

Read operator/DIRECTION_BRIEF.md (the LIVE brief) and operator/OPERATOR_LOG.md.
Interview me in short turns. Do not fill the verdict for me.

Walk fields 1 → 5:
1. Ask what I directed (confirm against the Brief).
2. Ask what I changed mid-run.
3. Ask my provisional verdict — then challenge weak reasoning without overriding me.
4. Demand concrete evidence (paths, checks, citations, reruns). Reject vibe language.
5. Ask what I still own.

When I accept the wording, write the entry into the correct block section in this file.
Remind me: REJECT with real evidence is a successful operator act.
```

## Transfer

Same pattern at work: after any serious AI-directed task, a five-field debrief with a thinking machine that interrogates your evidence — you still own the verdict.
