## Frozen-Reference status

The frozen Reference remains unchanged. Its bounds permit at most ten modules and 24–35 facilitated hours, so the current nine-module, 27-hour skeleton does not require a successor Reference.

## Remaining uncertainty

The current design can pass structural checks while delivery timing, case quality, responsibility/tool composition, accessibility, scoring, transfer, recipient capacity, makeups, and scale remain unmeasured.
