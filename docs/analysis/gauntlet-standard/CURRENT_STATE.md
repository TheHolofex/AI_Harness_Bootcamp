## Independence and qualification

Every module receives its own supplied case and machinery. No module consumes another module's evidence. A missed or held module does not block participation in the next session.

Final qualification still requires every outcome. Missing outcomes are reassessed at their original gate using an unseen protected case. Clean-session restartability and independent-person transfer remain separate.
