# P6 agentic diagrams

Date: 2026-08-06

## Brief

Add one overview and one figure at each of P6's five stages. Each figure must
show what Goose is doing and what the exercise demonstrates. Interest must
come from the changing mission and the consequential MPU-1 tradeoff, not from
capability restrictions or procedural complexity.

The final assets were generated with the built-in image-generation workflow,
then encoded as quality-92 WebP files for the course site.

## Common prompt

- Use case: scientific-educational technical figure.
- Composition: panoramic, full-frame diagram on `#0D0906`; no scene or staging.
- Palette: `#17110C`, `#A58650`, `#C8A96A`, `#C8B78A`, `#655337`,
  `#4F5634`, `#B43A2F`, and `#FFF8E7` only.
- Treatment: sharp dimensional panels, restrained warm traces, small emission
  dots, crisp all-caps sans-serif labels, and one clear hero element.
- Richness: warm, restrained, operational luminance. Not neon, cinematic,
  photographic, or a flat wireframe.
- Honesty: no invented metrics, percentages, gauges, timestamps, status values,
  or fake live data.
- Avoid: people, hands, monitors, desks, rooms, shields, locks, approval gates,
  watermarks, logos, and cool blue/cyan/purple colors.
- Render only the listed labels, exactly once and exactly as written.

## Final prompt set

### `p6-agentic-overview.webp`

Show `EVIDENCE` entering an `AGENTIC MISSION` loop of `INSPECT`, `DECIDE`,
`ACT`, `CHECK`, and `REVISE`. A `PERSISTED STATE` rail feeds every step.
`WORLD CHANGES` and `OPERATOR INTENT` enter the next pass. The checked exit is
`CURRENT OPERATING PRODUCT`. Keep verification inside the smaller `CHECK` step;
the output must not dominate the loop.

Hero: `AGENTIC MISSION`.

### `p6-stage-01-launch.webp`

Show `MIXED EVIDENCE` crossing `ONE LAUNCH` into `GOOSE OWNS THE JOB`.
Inside Goose, show the loop `INSPECT`, `CHOOSE`, `BUILD`, `CHECK`, `REPAIR`.
Emit two equal outputs: `COMMAND CENTER` and `MISSION STATE`.

Hero: `GOOSE OWNS THE JOB`.

### `p6-stage-02-command-center.webp`

Show `14 EVIDENCE ITEMS` entering `GOOSE SYNTHESIZES`. The bright
`FRONTSTAGE` output is `COMMAND CENTER`, with `PRIORITIES`, `NEXT ACTIONS`,
`MPU-1`, and `EVIDENCE`. The aligned `BACKSTAGE` output is `MISSION STATE`,
marked `SAME WATCH`.

Hero: `COMMAND CENTER`.

### `p6-stage-03-intent.webp`

Show `THE WORLD CHANGES` and `COLD STORE 7` releasing the single `ONE MPU-1`.
`YOUR OPERATING INTENT` feeds `INTENT STAGED FOR NEXT LAUNCH`. End in two
equal-weight, unresolved consequence paths: `BRIDGE FOXTROT` / `CLEAR BY 07:10`
and `RELAY KESTREL` / `ENERGIZED BYPASS` / `REGIONAL COORDINATION`. Use red only
for `CLEAR BY 07:10`. Do not show Goose acting, preselect either path, or
duplicate the resource.

Hero: `INTENT STAGED FOR NEXT LAUNCH`, supported by `ONE MPU-1`.

### `p6-stage-04-revise.webp`

Converge `WAVE 1 STATE`, `LATE EVIDENCE`, and `EXACT OPERATOR INTENT` into a
loop of `RECONCILE`, `REVISE`, and `CHECK`. Emit the `SAME COMMAND CENTER` with
`BEFORE / AFTER` and an `UPDATED MISSION STATE`. A change rail exposes `NEW`,
`CHANGED`, `CANCELLED`, and `UNCHANGED`. Arrowheads must read unambiguously as
`RECONCILE` → `REVISE` → `CHECK` → `RECONCILE`.

Hero: `SAME COMMAND CENTER`.

### `p6-stage-05-closeout.webp`

Map `P6 WATCH` through the invariant `AGENT MAINTAINS THE WORK` loop containing
`GOAL`, `DURABLE STATE`, `OPERATOR INTENT`, and `CHECKED REVISION`. Transfer the
pattern into `YOUR CHANGING QUEUE`, qualified by `NEW FACTS ARRIVE`,
`PRIORITIES SHIFT`, and `CONTINUITY MATTERS`.

Hero: `AGENT MAINTAINS THE WORK`.

## Verification

Every final figure was inspected after generation and again after WebP
encoding.

- Label fidelity: PASS. Every required label is present, correctly spelled,
  correctly placed, and unduplicated; no invented text is visible.
- No staging: PASS. The figures are full-frame diagrams without people,
  workstations, rooms, or cinematic treatment.
- Richness: PASS. Gradients, sheen, dimensional panels, and warm traces remain
  restrained and operational.
- Content honesty: PASS. The suite contains no invented operational metrics or
  fake live readouts.
- Palette: PASS. The figures use the warm-earth system; brick red appears only
  as a changed-world/deadline or cancelled-work accent.
- Composition: PASS. Each figure has one clear hero and a directional reading
  order. Stage 03 leaves both MPU-1 consequences visually unresolved.
- Sequence semantics: PASS. Stage 03 stages intent without claiming Goose has
  acted; Stage 04 shows the `RECONCILE` → `REVISE` → `CHECK` loop.
